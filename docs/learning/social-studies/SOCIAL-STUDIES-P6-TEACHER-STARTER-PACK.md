# Social Studies Teacher Starter Pack — Primary 6

**Curriculum:** Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), revised indicators where applicable
**Coverage:** 39 official indicator lessons
**Source alignment:** Each lesson retains the indicator code, learning standard and original Thai indicator wording from the curriculum database.

> **Classification note:** All objectives in this pack have an OBEC between-learning / terminal pathway classification. Where a 2021 Must-Know / Should-Know priority label is present, it is shown separately; the two classification systems are not interchangeable.

## Teaching guidance

Use the indicator as the learning target. Adapt examples to the learners' community and grade level. For religious content, respect the learner's faith and teach the curriculum's comparative language inclusively. For history and civics, distinguish evidence from opinion. For geography, use maps, local observations and reliable data. For economics, use familiar household and community examples.

## Indicator lessons

### 1. SO1.1-P6-01

- **Standard:** SO1.1
- **Priority classification:** should_know
- **OBEC pathway classification:** between_learning
- **Official indicator:** วิเคราะห์ความสำคัญของพระพุทธศาสนาในฐานะเป็นศาสนาประจำชาติ หรือความสำคัญของศาสนาที่ตนนับถือ

**Objective:** `SO1.1-P6-01` — วิเคราะห์ความสำคัญของพระพุทธศาสนาในฐานะเป็นศาสนาประจำชาติ หรือความสำคัญของศาสนาที่ตนนับถือ
- **Learning intention:** Learners will demonstrate: วิเคราะห์ความสำคัญของพระพุทธศาสนาในฐานะเป็นศาสนาประจำชาติ หรือความสำคัญของศาสนาที่ตนนับถือ
**Suggested teaching sequence**

1. Begin with a familiar situation involving kindness, honesty, responsibility or respectful coexistence; invite learners to describe the action without disclosing personal beliefs.
2. Present a short, balanced source about the relevant tradition, teaching or practice. Identify its context and distinguish factual information from personal opinion.
3. Model how the source connects to the indicator. In pairs, compare two possible actions and explain which better demonstrates the stated principle while respecting different beliefs.
4. Learners complete the application task independently and explain the evidence behind their choice. Assess understanding and respectful reasoning, never personal faith or worship.

**Resources:** a short age-appropriate account, a neutral information card and a real-life ethical situation.

**Differentiation:** For upper primary learners: Use short written directions, labelled visuals and a simple organiser; move from a modelled example to an independent response.

**Learning intention:** Learners will be able to demonstrate the specific knowledge or performance in this official indicator: วิเคราะห์ความสำคัญของพระพุทธศาสนาในฐานะเป็นศาสนาประจำชาติ หรือความสำคัญของศาสนาที่ตนนับถือ

**Practical activity:** Use a respectful account or case from an appropriate tradition; connect the relevant teaching, value or practice to a real-life choice without assessing personal belief. Adjust the complexity to upper primary learners and use a source or response format appropriate to their age.

**Expected evidence:** Three completed responses demonstrate SO1.1-P6-01: accurate identification, a clear explanation with a relevant example, and a grade-appropriate application task. Accept oral, written, visual, map-based, timeline-based or practical evidence where it measures the same target.

**Teacher check:** Check separately (1) accuracy against the Thai indicator, (2) explanation supported by an example or source, and (3) application to the scenario. Give feedback on the missing criterion.

**Independent practice**
1. **Recall / identify:** Use the **Community faith-and-service information card**. A teacher-prepared, balanced card describes how a religious tradition influences community service, ethical choices and shared celebrations; it identifies the source and avoids claiming that every citizen shares one belief. identify two distinct ways the source links a tradition with community life. This assesses “วิเคราะห์ความสำคัญของพระพุทธศาสนาในฐานะเป็นศาสนาประจำชาติ หรือความสำคัญของศาสนาที่ตนนับถือ”. **Answer:** the two links are accurately located in the supplied card and distinguished from opinion.
2. **Explain / analyse:** Using the **Community faith-and-service information card**, explain how one source detail supports the indicator’s analysis of religious significance. Support your response with evidence from the supplied material. This assesses “วิเคราะห์ความสำคัญของพระพุทธศาสนาในฐานะเป็นศาสนาประจำชาติ หรือความสำคัญของศาสนาที่ตนนับถือ”. **Answer:** the explanation connects a stated source detail to a reasoned social or ethical significance without generalising about all people.
3. **Apply / demonstrate:** Using the **Community faith-and-service information card**, write a respectful comparison statement for a mixed-belief classroom. Explain your decision or show the result. This assesses “วิเคราะห์ความสำคัญของพระพุทธศาสนาในฐานะเป็นศาสนาประจำชาติ หรือความสำคัญของศาสนาที่ตนนับถือ”. **Answer:** the statement recognises the tradition discussed, avoids ranking beliefs and supports coexistence.

This assesses “วิเคราะห์การเปลี่ยนแปลงทางกายภาพของประเทศไทยในอดีตกับปัจจุบัน และผลที่เกิดขึ้นจากการเปลี่ยนแปลง”. **Answer:** response identifies evidence, a supported effect and one further source needed to confirm interpretation.
