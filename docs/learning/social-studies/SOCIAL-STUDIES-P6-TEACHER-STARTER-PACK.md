# Social Studies Teacher Starter Pack — Primary 6

**Curriculum:** Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), revised indicators where applicable
**Coverage:** 39 official indicator lessons
**Source alignment:** Each lesson retains the indicator code, learning standard and original Thai indicator wording from the curriculum database.

> Classification labels are shown as currently stored. Items marked “Classification audit pending” are not presented as verified; the subject audit is reconciling these against OBEC's between-learning / terminal indicator framework.

## Teaching guidance

Use the indicator as the learning target. Adapt examples to the learners' community and grade level. For religious content, respect the learner's faith and teach the curriculum's comparative language inclusively. For history and civics, distinguish evidence from opinion. For geography, use maps, local observations and reliable data. For economics, use familiar household and community examples.

### 1. SO1.1-P6-01

- **Standard:** SO1.1
- **Priority classification:** should_know
- **Official indicator:** วิเคราะห์ความสำคัญของพระพุทธศาสนาในฐานะเป็นศาสนาประจำชาติ หรือความสำคัญของศาสนาที่ตนนับถือ
- **Learning intention:** Learners demonstrate the knowledge or performance stated in this indicator.

**Suggested teaching sequence**
1. Activate prior knowledge with a familiar home, school, community, Thai-history, civic, economic, religious or geographical example relevant to the indicator.
2. Model the key idea using a short explanation, visual, source, map, timeline, case study or practical example.
3. Guide learners to practise the indicator in pairs or small groups.
4. Check individual understanding before the lesson ends.

**Assessment prompts**
1. **Recall / identify:** Ask learners to state, identify or describe the key knowledge in their own words: วิเคราะห์ความสำคัญของพระพุทธศาสนาในฐานะเป็นศาสนาประจำชาติ หรือความสำคัญของศาสนาที่ตนนับถือ
2. **Explain / analyse:** Ask learners to explain why or how the indicator matters, using one accurate example.
3. **Apply / demonstrate:** Give a short, age-appropriate real-life or source-based task requiring learners to demonstrate the indicator.

**Expected evidence:** An accurate oral, written, visual, map-based, timeline-based or practical response that directly demonstrates the indicator.

**Teacher check:** Record whether the learner can (a) identify the key concept, (b) explain it accurately, and (c) apply or demonstrate it independently.
