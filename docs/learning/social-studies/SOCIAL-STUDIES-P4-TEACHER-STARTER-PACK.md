# Social Studies Teacher Starter Pack — Primary 4

**Curriculum:** Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), revised indicators where applicable
**Coverage:** 38 official indicator lessons
**Source alignment:** Each lesson retains the indicator code, learning standard and original Thai indicator wording from the curriculum database.

> **Classification note:** All objectives in this pack have an OBEC between-learning / terminal pathway classification. Where a 2021 Must-Know / Should-Know priority label is present, it is shown separately; the two classification systems are not interchangeable.

## Teaching guidance

Use the indicator as the learning target. Adapt examples to the learners' community and grade level. For religious content, respect the learner's faith and teach the curriculum's comparative language inclusively. For history and civics, distinguish evidence from opinion. For geography, use maps, local observations and reliable data. For economics, use familiar household and community examples.

## Indicator lessons

### 1. SO1.1-P4-01

- **Standard:** SO1.1
- **Priority classification:** should_know
- **OBEC pathway classification:** between_learning
- **Official indicator:** อธิบายความสำคัญของพระพุทธศาสนาหรือศาสนาที่ตนนับถือในฐานะเป็นศูนย์รวมจิตใจของศาสนิกชน

**Objective:** `SO1.1-P4-01` — อธิบายความสำคัญของพระพุทธศาสนาหรือศาสนาที่ตนนับถือในฐานะเป็นศูนย์รวมจิตใจของศาสนิกชน
- **Learning intention:** Learners will demonstrate: อธิบายความสำคัญของพระพุทธศาสนาหรือศาสนาที่ตนนับถือในฐานะเป็นศูนย์รวมจิตใจของศาสนิกชน
**Suggested teaching sequence**

1. Begin with a familiar situation involving kindness, honesty, responsibility or respectful coexistence; invite learners to describe the action without disclosing personal beliefs.
2. Present a short, balanced source about the relevant tradition, teaching or practice. Identify its context and distinguish factual information from personal opinion.
3. Model how the source connects to the indicator. In pairs, compare two possible actions and explain which better demonstrates the stated principle while respecting different beliefs.
4. Learners complete the application task independently and explain the evidence behind their choice. Assess understanding and respectful reasoning, never personal faith or worship.

**Resources:** a short age-appropriate account, a neutral information card and a real-life ethical situation.

**Differentiation:** For upper primary learners: Use short written directions, labelled visuals and a simple organiser; move from a modelled example to an independent response.

**Learning intention:** Learners will be able to demonstrate the specific knowledge or performance in this official indicator: อธิบายความสำคัญของพระพุทธศาสนาหรือศาสนาที่ตนนับถือในฐานะเป็นศูนย์รวมจิตใจของศาสนิกชน

**Practical activity:** Use a respectful account or case from an appropriate tradition; connect the relevant teaching, value or practice to a real-life choice without assessing personal belief. Adjust the complexity to upper primary learners and use a source or response format appropriate to their age.

**Expected evidence:** Three completed responses demonstrate SO1.1-P4-01: accurate identification, a clear explanation with a relevant example, and a grade-appropriate application task. Accept oral, written, visual, map-based, timeline-based or practical evidence where it measures the same target.

**Teacher check:** Check separately (1) accuracy against the Thai indicator, (2) explanation supported by an example or source, and (3) application to the scenario. Give feedback on the missing criterion.

**Assessment stimulus — shared religious community:** Approved source card: members gather at a place of worship, learn shared teachings, support one another and help neighbours.

**Independent practice**
1. **Recall / identify:** Read this shared religious community card: Approved source card: members gather at a place of worship, learn shared teachings, support one another and help neighbours. Identify two accurate details relevant to this indicator: “อธิบายความสำคัญของพระพุทธศาสนาหรือศาสนาที่ตนนับถือในฐานะเป็นศูนย์รวมจิตใจของศาสนิกชน”. **Answer:** gathering to learn; helping neighbours Accept equivalent details supported by the card.
2. **Explain / analyse:** Explain what the shared religious community card shows and why it matters for this indicator: “อธิบายความสำคัญของพระพุทธศาสนาหรือศาสนาที่ตนนับถือในฐานะเป็นศูนย์รวมจิตใจของศาสนิกชน”. Use one detail as evidence. **Answer:** A shared place or teaching gives members a place to meet, learn values and support one another. Award credit only when explanation is linked to evidence.
3. **Apply / demonstrate:** Describe one respectful way to support a classmate whose family follows a different belief. This assesses: “อธิบายความสำคัญของพระพุทธศาสนาหรือศาสนาที่ตนนับถือในฐานะเป็นศูนย์รวมจิตใจของศาสนิกชน”. **Answer:** A respectful response listens, avoids pressure or teasing and offers help. Award credit for a suitable response supported by the card.
