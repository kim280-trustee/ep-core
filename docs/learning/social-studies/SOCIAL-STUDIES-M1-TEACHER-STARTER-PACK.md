# Social Studies Teacher Starter Pack — Lower Secondary 1

**Curriculum:** Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), revised indicators where applicable
**Coverage:** 45 official indicator lessons
**Source alignment:** Each lesson retains the indicator code, learning standard and original Thai indicator wording from the curriculum database.

> **Classification note:** All objectives in this pack have an OBEC between-learning / terminal pathway classification. Where a 2021 Must-Know / Should-Know priority label is present, it is shown separately; the two classification systems are not interchangeable.

## Teaching guidance

Use the indicator as the learning target. Adapt examples to the learners' community and grade level. For religious content, respect the learner's faith and teach the curriculum's comparative language inclusively. For history and civics, distinguish evidence from opinion. For geography, use maps, local observations and reliable data. For economics, use familiar household and community examples.

## Indicator lessons

### 1. SO1.1-M1-01

- **Standard:** SO1.1
- **Priority classification:** should_know
- **OBEC pathway classification:** between_learning
- **Official indicator:** อธิบายการเผยแผ่พระพุทธศาสนาหรือศาสนาที่ตนนับถือสู่ประเทศไทย

**Objective:** `SO1.1-M1-01` — อธิบายการเผยแผ่พระพุทธศาสนาหรือศาสนาที่ตนนับถือสู่ประเทศไทย
- **Learning intention:** Learners will demonstrate: อธิบายการเผยแผ่พระพุทธศาสนาหรือศาสนาที่ตนนับถือสู่ประเทศไทย
**Suggested teaching sequence**

1. Begin with a familiar situation involving kindness, honesty, responsibility or respectful coexistence; invite learners to describe the action without disclosing personal beliefs.
2. Present a short, balanced source about the relevant tradition, teaching or practice. Identify its context and distinguish factual information from personal opinion.
3. Model how the source connects to the indicator. In pairs, compare two possible actions and explain which better demonstrates the stated principle while respecting different beliefs.
4. Learners complete the application task independently and explain the evidence behind their choice. Assess understanding and respectful reasoning, never personal faith or worship.

**Resources:** a short age-appropriate account, a neutral information card and a real-life ethical situation.

**Differentiation:** For lower secondary learners: Use short authentic sources and guided vocabulary; require learners to explain their reasoning and cite details from the case or source.

**Learning intention:** Learners will be able to demonstrate the specific knowledge or performance in this official indicator: อธิบายการเผยแผ่พระพุทธศาสนาหรือศาสนาที่ตนนับถือสู่ประเทศไทย

**Practical activity:** Use a short, respectful account or case from an appropriate tradition. Identify the relevant teaching, value or practice and connect it to a real-life choice; never assess personal belief or require worship. Adapt the source and response length to lower secondary learners, using pictures and oral modelling for early primary, short organisers for upper primary, guided source analysis for lower secondary, and multiple sources or data for upper secondary.

**Expected evidence:** Three completed responses that demonstrate SO1.1-M1-01: accurate identification, a clear explanation supported by a relevant example, and a grade-appropriate application task. Accept oral, written, visual, map-based, timeline-based or practical evidence when it measures the same learning target.

**Teacher check:** Check separately: (1) accuracy against the Thai indicator, (2) explanation supported by a relevant example or source, and (3) application to the scenario. Give feedback on the missing criterion; do not reward unsupported guessing.

**Independent practice**
1. **Recall / identify:** From the lesson's picture, short source, map or classroom example, identify 2 accurate facts, terms, people, practices or features needed to meet this indicator: “อธิบายการเผยแผ่พระพุทธศาสนาหรือศาสนาที่ตนนับถือสู่ประเทศไทย”. Answer in a short list, labelled drawing or oral response. **Answer:** Identify 2 accurate items directly required by official indicator SO1.1-M1-01 (อธิบายการเผยแผ่พระพุทธศาสนาหรือศาสนาที่ตนนับถือสู่ประเทศไทย). Accept equivalent wording and age-appropriate examples; do not award unrelated facts.
2. **Explain / analyse:** In your own words, explain how or why the idea in this indicator matters in religion, ethics and ways of life: “อธิบายการเผยแผ่พระพุทธศาสนาหรือศาสนาที่ตนนับถือสู่ประเทศไทย”. Support your explanation with one relevant Thai or local example and explain how the example demonstrates the indicator. **Answer:** Accurately explain the idea or relationship stated in SO1.1-M1-01 and support it with one relevant example. Accept other accurate examples; the explanation must address the indicator rather than repeat it.
3. **Apply / demonstrate:** Analyse a short school or community case connected to “อธิบายการเผยแผ่พระพุทธศาสนาหรือศาสนาที่ตนนับถือสู่ประเทศไทย”. Identify the relevant teaching, value or practice, compare two possible responses, and justify the more respectful response. **Answer:** Identify a relevant principle from SO1.1-M1-01, compare both responses fairly and justify a respectful application. Do not grade personal belief.
