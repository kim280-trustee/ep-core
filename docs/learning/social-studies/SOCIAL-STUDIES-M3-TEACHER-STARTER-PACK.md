# Social Studies Teacher Starter Pack — Lower Secondary 3

**Curriculum:** Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), revised indicators where applicable
**Coverage:** 50 official indicator lessons
**Source alignment:** Each lesson retains the indicator code, learning standard and original Thai indicator wording from the curriculum database.

> **Classification note:** All objectives in this pack have an OBEC between-learning / terminal pathway classification. Where a 2021 Must-Know / Should-Know priority label is present, it is shown separately; the two classification systems are not interchangeable.

## Teaching guidance

Use the indicator as the learning target. Adapt examples to the learners' community and grade level. For religious content, respect the learner's faith and teach the curriculum's comparative language inclusively. For history and civics, distinguish evidence from opinion. For geography, use maps, local observations and reliable data. For economics, use familiar household and community examples.

## Indicator lessons

### 1. SO1.1-M3-01

- **Standard:** SO1.1
- **OBEC pathway classification:** between_learning
- **Official indicator:** อธิบายการเผยแผ่พระพุทธศาสนาหรือศาสนาที่ตนนับถือสู่ประเทศต่าง ๆ ทั่วโลก

**Objective:** `SO1.1-M3-01` — อธิบายการเผยแผ่พระพุทธศาสนาหรือศาสนาที่ตนนับถือสู่ประเทศต่าง ๆ ทั่วโลก
- **Learning intention:** Learners will demonstrate: อธิบายการเผยแผ่พระพุทธศาสนาหรือศาสนาที่ตนนับถือสู่ประเทศต่าง ๆ ทั่วโลก
**Suggested teaching sequence**

1. Introduce the exact religious, historical, ethical or cultural focus named in the indicator through a balanced, age-appropriate source. Make clear that learners are studying curriculum knowledge, not being asked to disclose personal belief.
2. Model source reading: identify origin, context, key terms, people or events, and distinguish documented information from interpretation. Where relevant, compare accounts or traditions respectfully.
3. Learners investigate the specific focus in the indicator: historical spread, cultural contribution, sustainability, biography, doctrine, ethical decision, religious practice, interfaith understanding or community responsibility.
4. Learners present an evidence-based explanation or an appropriate, inclusive application. Offer a non-devotional equivalent for ritual or identity-related outcomes; never assess personal faith or require disclosure.

**Resources:** a balanced religious-history or cultural source, a relevant text/teaching extract or case, and a source-analysis or comparison organiser.

**Differentiation:** For M3 learners: Pre-teach subject vocabulary and provide a structured source/data organiser. Support learners with a shortened, clearly labelled source and guided prompts; extend learners by corroborating sources, evaluating competing explanations, comparing perspectives or defending a conclusion with multiple pieces of evidence.

**Learning intention:** Learners will be able to demonstrate the specific knowledge or performance in this official indicator: อธิบายการเผยแผ่พระพุทธศาสนาหรือศาสนาที่ตนนับถือสู่ประเทศต่าง ๆ ทั่วโลก

**Practical activity:** Learners investigate the precise religious-history, teaching, ethical, cultural or social focus named in the indicator. They use a neutral source to explain or analyse it and complete an inclusive application task. Any ritual or identity-related learning must have a non-devotional equivalent; never require personal belief disclosure.
**Expected evidence:** Three completed responses that demonstrate SO1.1-M3-01: accurate identification, a clear explanation supported by a relevant example, and a grade-appropriate application task. Accept oral, written, visual, map-based, timeline-based or practical evidence when it measures the same learning target.

**Teacher check:** Check separately: (1) accuracy against the Thai indicator, (2) explanation supported by a relevant example or source, and (3) application to the scenario. Give feedback on the missing criterion; do not reward unsupported guessing.

**Independent practice**
1. **Recall / identify:** Using the neutral, age-appropriate source supplied for this lesson, identify two accurate facts about the routes, people, periods and social contexts through which the named religion spread internationally. Record the source detail supporting each fact. This assesses: “อธิบายการเผยแผ่พระพุทธศาสนาหรือศาสนาที่ตนนับถือสู่ประเทศต่าง ๆ ทั่วโลก”. **Answer:** Identifies two accurate, relevant facts about the routes, people, periods and social contexts through which the named religion spread internationally, each supported by the supplied source and directly connected to SO1.1-M3-01.
2. **Explain / analyse:** Analyse why the routes, people, periods and social contexts through which the named religion spread internationally matters. Explain one important relationship, contribution, difference or consequence, as appropriate to the indicator, and support your reasoning with at least two source details. This assesses: “อธิบายการเผยแผ่พระพุทธศาสนาหรือศาสนาที่ตนนับถือสู่ประเทศต่าง ๆ ทั่วโลก”. **Answer:** Explains the significance or relationship accurately, uses at least two relevant source details and makes a defensible conclusion. Accept alternative interpretations supported by evidence.
3. **Apply / demonstrate:** Apply the learning about the routes, people, periods and social contexts through which the named religion spread internationally to the short school or community case. Present a respectful, evidence-based response or plan and explain why it fits the indicator. For any ritual or religious-identity outcome, use an informed simulation or equivalent non-devotional response; do not disclose personal faith. This assesses: “อธิบายการเผยแผ่พระพุทธศาสนาหรือศาสนาที่ตนนับถือสู่ประเทศต่าง ๆ ทั่วโลก”. **Answer:** Gives an accurate, respectful application or plan linked to SO1.1-M3-01 and justifies it with evidence. Do not assess personal belief, worship or disclosure; provide an equivalent non-devotional route where relevant.

**Learning intention:** Learners will be able to demonstrate the specific knowledge or performance in this official indicator: ระบุความร่วมมือระหว่างประเทศที่มีผลต่อการจัดการทรัพยากรและสิ่งแวดล้อม

**Practical activity:** Learners investigate the specified North or South American location, pattern or environmental issue using thematic maps and geographic evidence. They produce an annotated map, spatial analysis or justified sustainable-management recommendation.
**Expected evidence:** Three completed responses that demonstrate SO5.2-M3-05: accurate identification, a clear explanation supported by a relevant example, and a grade-appropriate application task. Accept oral, written, visual, map-based, timeline-based or practical evidence when it measures the same learning target.

**Teacher check:** Check separately: (1) accuracy against the Thai indicator, (2) explanation supported by a relevant example or source, and (3) application to the scenario. Give feedback on the missing criterion; do not reward unsupported guessing.

**Independent practice**
1. **Recall / identify:** Use the thematic map, atlas and key to identify two accurate geographic facts about international cooperation that affects resource and environmental management. Include relevant place names, direction, distribution or map evidence. This assesses: “ระบุความร่วมมือระหว่างประเทศที่มีผลต่อการจัดการทรัพยากรและสิ่งแวดล้อม”. **Answer:** Correctly identifies two relevant geographic facts and locations, using map keys, direction or distribution accurately where required.
2. **Explain / analyse:** Analyse international cooperation that affects resource and environmental management by explaining the relationship between location, physical conditions and/or human activity. Use at least two map, photograph or data details to support the explanation. This assesses: “ระบุความร่วมมือระหว่างประเทศที่มีผลต่อการจัดการทรัพยากรและสิ่งแวดล้อม”. **Answer:** Explains the specified spatial or human-environment relationship and supports it with at least two accurate geographic details.
3. **Apply / demonstrate:** Create an annotated map, spatial analysis or sustainable-management proposal addressing international cooperation that affects resource and environmental management. Identify the places or groups affected, use geographic evidence and justify the proposed response. This assesses: “ระบุความร่วมมือระหว่างประเทศที่มีผลต่อการจัดการทรัพยากรและสิ่งแวดล้อม”. **Answer:** Produces an accurate map, analysis or feasible recommendation that identifies affected places/groups and justifies the response with geographic evidence.
