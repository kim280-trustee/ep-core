# Social Studies Teacher Starter Pack — Lower Secondary 2

**Curriculum:** Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), revised indicators where applicable
**Coverage:** 45 official indicator lessons
**Source alignment:** Each lesson retains the indicator code, learning standard and original Thai indicator wording from the curriculum database.

> **Classification note:** All objectives in this pack have an OBEC between-learning / terminal pathway classification. Where a 2021 Must-Know / Should-Know priority label is present, it is shown separately; the two classification systems are not interchangeable.

## Teaching guidance

Use the indicator as the learning target. Adapt examples to the learners' community and grade level. For religious content, respect the learner's faith and teach the curriculum's comparative language inclusively. For history and civics, distinguish evidence from opinion. For geography, use maps, local observations and reliable data. For economics, use familiar household and community examples.

## Indicator lessons

### 1. SO1.1-M2-01

- **Standard:** SO1.1
- **Priority classification:** should_know
- **OBEC pathway classification:** between_learning
- **Official indicator:** อธิบายการเผยแผ่พระพุทธศาสนาหรือศาสนาที่ตนนับถือสู่ประเทศเพื่อนบ้าน

**Objective:** `SO1.1-M2-01` — อธิบายการเผยแผ่พระพุทธศาสนาหรือศาสนาที่ตนนับถือสู่ประเทศเพื่อนบ้าน
- **Learning intention:** Learners will demonstrate: อธิบายการเผยแผ่พระพุทธศาสนาหรือศาสนาที่ตนนับถือสู่ประเทศเพื่อนบ้าน
**Suggested teaching sequence**

1. Introduce the exact religious-history, teaching, text or practice named in the indicator through a neutral, age-appropriate source. Invite learners to identify what the source states without asking them to disclose personal beliefs.
2. Model how to identify the source context, key terms and relevant details. Clarify the distinction between curriculum knowledge, historical evidence and personal opinion.
3. Learners analyse the source in pairs, organising details around the indicator's specific focus: chronology, teaching, text structure, practice, cultural influence or community relationship as applicable.
4. Learners complete an individual source-based response or respectful application. Assess accuracy, evidence and reasoning; never assess personal faith, worship or disclosure.

**Resources:** a short age-appropriate account, a neutral information card and a relevant curriculum source extract.

**Differentiation:** For lower secondary learners: Use a short, authentic source or case, pre-teach essential vocabulary, provide a structured evidence organiser, and require learners to justify conclusions with relevant details. Extend learners by asking them to compare sources, consider alternative explanations or evaluate consequences.

**Learning intention:** Learners will be able to demonstrate the specific knowledge or performance in this official indicator: อธิบายการเผยแผ่พระพุทธศาสนาหรือศาสนาที่ตนนับถือสู่ประเทศเพื่อนบ้าน

**Practical activity:** Use a short, neutral source directly matched to the indicator. Learners identify its key details, explain the relevant historical, ethical, textual or social meaning, and apply the learning to a suitable scenario. Offer an equivalent non-devotional response; never require worship or disclosure of personal belief.
**Expected evidence:** Three completed responses that demonstrate SO1.1-M2-01: accurate identification, a clear explanation supported by a relevant example, and a grade-appropriate application task. Accept oral, written, visual, map-based, timeline-based or practical evidence when it measures the same learning target.

**Teacher check:** Check separately: (1) accuracy against the Thai indicator, (2) explanation supported by a relevant example or source, and (3) application to the scenario. Give feedback on the missing criterion; do not reward unsupported guessing.

**Independent practice**
1. **Recall / identify:** Use the short, neutral source card prepared for this lesson. Identify two accurate details about the social, cultural or community relationship named in the indicator. State where each detail appears in the source. This assesses: “อธิบายการเผยแผ่พระพุทธศาสนาหรือศาสนาที่ตนนับถือสู่ประเทศเพื่อนบ้าน”. **Answer:** Names two accurate, relevant details about the social, cultural or community relationship named in the indicator; both must be supported by the supplied source and directly address SO1.1-M2-01.
2. **Explain / analyse:** Explain the significance or relationship expressed in the indicator. Support your explanation with two details from the source and one relevant example; distinguish source-based facts from your interpretation. This assesses: “อธิบายการเผยแผ่พระพุทธศาสนาหรือศาสนาที่ตนนับถือสู่ประเทศเพื่อนบ้าน”. **Answer:** Explains the indicator's central significance or relationship accurately, uses two relevant source details and connects them to a suitable example. Accept other defensible examples supported by evidence.
3. **Apply / demonstrate:** Respond to a realistic school or community scenario that requires the knowledge in this indicator. Select or demonstrate an appropriate response, explain why it fits the source or teaching, and show respect for people with different beliefs. Do not disclose personal faith or perform worship. This assesses: “อธิบายการเผยแผ่พระพุทธศาสนาหรือศาสนาที่ตนนับถือสู่ประเทศเพื่อนบ้าน”. **Answer:** Applies the relevant knowledge accurately to the scenario, gives a clear reason and demonstrates respectful inclusion. Assess understanding and reasoning, never personal belief, worship or disclosure of faith.
