# Career Education — P6 to M4–M6 Teacher Starter Pack

**Curriculum:** Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), applicable revisions  
**Learning area:** การงานอาชีพ (Career Education)  
**Coverage:** P6 (5 indicators), M1 (6), M2 (6), M3 (6), M4–M6 shared indicators (11)  
**Resource type:** Original teacher-created starter lessons aligned to the platform's registered indicator records.

## Teaching and safeguarding guidance
Use supervised, age-appropriate practical tasks. Electrical, chemical, sharp-tool, food-safety and machinery activities must be demonstrated or directly supervised by a qualified adult; learners should not operate hazardous equipment. For career exploration, use public information or voluntary interviews with permission. Do not require learners to disclose family income, private circumstances or personal contact details. M4–M6 objectives are shared interval indicators and are mapped to M4, M5 and M6 in the curriculum database; adapt depth and independence across the three years.

# P6 — Planning, teamwork and career self-awareness

## Lesson 1 — Improving each step of a task
**Indicator:** CAREER-P6-01 — อภิปรายแนวทางในการทำงานและปรับปรุงการทำงานแต่ละขั้นตอน  
**Outcomes:** Review a task sequence, identify a weak step and suggest an improvement.  
**Starter:** Show a sequence for preparing a class display with one step missing. Ask learners to identify the problem.  
**Activity:** Teams plan, perform and review a classroom organisation task, recording one improvement for each stage.  
**Evidence:** Explains a work sequence and a realistic improvement.  
**Questions**
1. Why should a work process be reviewed? **Answer:** To identify problems and improve the next attempt.
2. What information helps you decide which step needs improvement? **Answer:** The plan, observations, time taken and quality of the result.
3. Give one example of improving a classroom task. **Answer:** Label storage areas so materials can be found and returned more quickly.

## Lesson 2 — Managing work and working together
**Indicator:** CAREER-P6-02 — ใช้ทักษะการจัดการในการทำงานและมีทักษะการทำงานร่วมกัน  
**Outcomes:** Allocate roles, manage time and resources, and coordinate team progress.  
**Starter:** Present a group task with four jobs and ask learners how to share them fairly.  
**Activity:** Groups organise a class resource station with a role chart and time plan.  
**Evidence:** Roles are clear, resources are managed and learners communicate progress.  
**Questions**
1. What should a group agree before starting? **Answer:** The goal, roles, steps, resources and time.
2. Why should roles match members' abilities? **Answer:** It helps each person contribute effectively and safely.
3. What should a group do if one task is delayed? **Answer:** Discuss the cause and adjust the plan or provide support.

## Lesson 3 — Working politely with family and others
**Indicator:** CAREER-P6-03 — ปฏิบัติตนอย่างมีมารยาทในการทำงานกับครอบครัวและผู้อื่น  
**Outcomes:** Demonstrate respectful communication, sharing and turn-taking.  
**Starter:** Role-play a disagreement over shared materials. Ask learners to suggest respectful responses.  
**Activity:** Create a fictional shared-responsibility agreement for a classroom or community activity.  
**Evidence:** Uses polite language, respects turns and shares resources.  
**Questions**
1. What is one way to show good manners when sharing tools? **Answer:** Ask permission, take turns and return tools.
2. How can you resolve a disagreement respectfully? **Answer:** Listen, explain calmly and agree on a fair solution.
3. Why should people respect a queue or agreed order? **Answer:** It treats people fairly and prevents conflict.

## Lesson 4 — Exploring yourself when considering careers
**Indicator:** CAREER-P6-04 — สำรวจตนเองเพื่อวางแผนในการเลือกอาชีพ  
**Outcomes:** Identify interests, strengths and values; connect them cautiously to career areas.  
**Starter:** Learners choose from interest cards such as helping, designing, repairing, growing and organising.  
**Activity:** Complete a private self-exploration worksheet; learners choose what, if anything, they share.  
**Evidence:** Identifies personal interests and one area to explore further.  
**Questions**
1. Name two things to consider when exploring a career. **Answer:** Interests and abilities; values and preferred work activities are also relevant.
2. Why should career choices not be based only on popularity? **Answer:** A suitable choice should also fit interests, abilities and values.
3. Can interests and abilities change over time? **Answer:** Yes. Learning and experience can develop them.

## Lesson 5 — Knowledge, abilities and values for an occupation
**Indicator:** CAREER-P6-05 — ระบุความรู้ความสามารถและคุณธรรมที่สัมพันธ์กับอาชีพที่สนใจ  
**Outcomes:** Match occupations with relevant knowledge, skills and ethical qualities.  
**Starter:** Compare the skills and values needed by a nurse, farmer and mechanic.  
**Activity:** Build an occupation profile showing tasks, knowledge, skills and ethical responsibilities.  
**Evidence:** Gives accurate examples of knowledge, ability and values for one occupation.  
**Questions**
1. What knowledge might a nurse need? **Answer:** Health and care knowledge.
2. Why is honesty important in many occupations? **Answer:** It builds trust and supports responsible decisions.
3. Name one skill useful in most workplaces. **Answer:** Communication, teamwork or problem-solving.

# M1 — Work process, group responsibility and career awareness

## Lesson 6 — Analysing a work process
**Indicator:** CAREER-M1-01 — วิเคราะห์ขั้นตอนการทำงานตามกระบวนการทำงาน  
**Outcomes:** Set a goal, assign roles, plan, perform, evaluate and improve.  
**Starter:** Give a scenario where a group project missed its deadline. Ask what process failed.  
**Activity:** Teams analyse and redesign the workflow for a school event preparation task.  
**Evidence:** Identifies process stages and explains how they connect.  
**Questions**
1. What should be defined before a group starts work? **Answer:** The goal and expected result.
2. Why should tasks be divided according to ability? **Answer:** To use skills effectively and complete work safely.
3. What is the purpose of evaluating a completed task? **Answer:** To identify strengths and improvements.

## Lesson 7 — Contributing to group work with sacrifice
**Indicator:** CAREER-M1-02 — ใช้กระบวนการกลุ่มในการทำงานด้วยความเสียสละ  
**Outcomes:** Put team goals into practice, support others and share responsibility.  
**Starter:** Discuss a scenario where one member finishes early while another needs help.  
**Activity:** Complete a team challenge with rotating roles and a short reflection on cooperation.  
**Evidence:** Supports group progress without taking over others' responsibilities.  
**Questions**
1. What does contributing to group success mean? **Answer:** Completing your role and helping the team when appropriate.
2. Why should group members share resources? **Answer:** It allows everyone to complete their tasks.
3. How can a team respond when a member needs support? **Answer:** Offer appropriate help and adjust tasks fairly.

## Lesson 8 — Making reasoned decisions to solve work problems
**Indicator:** CAREER-M1-03 — ตัดสินใจแก้ปัญหาการทำงานอย่างมีเหตุผล  
**Outcomes:** Define a problem, compare solutions and justify a decision.  
**Starter:** Present a task with limited materials and ask learners to suggest possible responses.  
**Activity:** Teams use a problem-solving chart: problem, causes, options, criteria, decision and review.  
**Evidence:** Chooses a solution supported by reasons.  
**Questions**
1. Why should the cause of a problem be identified? **Answer:** To choose a solution that addresses the real issue.
2. What should be compared when choosing between solutions? **Answer:** Safety, time, cost, resources and likely results.
3. Why should a solution be reviewed after use? **Answer:** To see whether it worked and what should change.

## Lesson 9 — Explaining career-choice approaches
**Indicator:** CAREER-M1-04 — อธิบายแนวทางการเลือกอาชีพ  
**Outcomes:** Identify career information sources and consider interests, abilities and ethical work.  
**Starter:** Ask learners where reliable career information can be found.  
**Activity:** Compare two career profiles using duties, qualifications, work setting and values.  
**Evidence:** Explains a sensible process for exploring an occupation.  
**Questions**
1. Name two sources of reliable career information. **Answer:** Official career guidance and verified education or occupational profiles.
2. Why should ethical considerations matter in career choice? **Answer:** Work should be lawful, responsible and respectful of others.
3. What should be checked before accepting online career information? **Answer:** The source, date, evidence and possible bias.

## Lesson 10 — Developing a positive attitude toward work
**Indicator:** CAREER-M1-05 — มีเจตคติที่ดีต่อการประกอบอาชีพ  
**Outcomes:** Recognise the value of honest work and respect different occupations.  
**Starter:** Learners identify contributions made by workers in their community.  
**Activity:** Create a “work contributes” poster featuring several occupations and their services.  
**Evidence:** Describes occupations respectfully and identifies their contribution.  
**Questions**
1. What is one sign of a positive attitude toward work? **Answer:** Responsibility and willingness to learn.
2. Why should honest occupations be respected? **Answer:** They provide useful goods or services and support society.
3. Does every occupation require the same skills? **Answer:** No. Different jobs require different knowledge and abilities.

## Lesson 11 — Understanding the importance of creating employment
**Indicator:** CAREER-M1-06 — เห็นความสำคัญของการสร้างอาชีพ  
**Outcomes:** Explain how occupations support individuals, households and communities.  
**Starter:** Map goods and services used in one ordinary day and identify occupations behind them.  
**Activity:** Groups design a simple community service idea and identify who benefits.  
**Evidence:** Explains how work can meet a need and create value.  
**Questions**
1. How can creating an occupation benefit a community? **Answer:** It can provide needed services and livelihood opportunities.
2. What should be understood before starting a small service? **Answer:** The need, skills, resources, safety and responsibilities involved.
3. Why should a service consider community needs? **Answer:** To provide something useful and appropriate.

# M2 — Research, problem solving and preparation for work

## Lesson 12 — Finding knowledge to improve work
**Indicator:** CAREER-M2-01 — ใช้ทักษะการแสวงหาความรู้เพื่อพัฒนาการทำงาน  
**Outcomes:** Search, observe, collect, record and apply relevant information.  
**Starter:** Ask how a learner could find a better method for organising a study area.  
**Activity:** Research two methods using teacher-approved sources and compare their usefulness.  
**Evidence:** Records sources and applies relevant findings.  
**Questions**
1. Name two ways to gather information. **Answer:** Observation and research; interviews or surveys are also possible.
2. Why should information sources be recorded? **Answer:** So information can be checked and credited.
3. How can research improve a work process? **Answer:** It can reveal safer, more efficient or more effective methods.

## Lesson 13 — Applying problem-solving skills at work
**Indicator:** CAREER-M2-02 — ใช้ทักษะกระบวนการแก้ปัญหาในการทำงาน  
**Outcomes:** Define a work problem, identify causes, generate options and evaluate outcomes.  
**Starter:** Present a scenario where a project repeatedly runs out of materials.  
**Activity:** Use a cause-and-effect diagram and propose a measurable improvement.  
**Evidence:** Explains the cause and justifies a solution.  
**Questions**
1. What is the difference between a problem and a cause? **Answer:** The problem is the issue; the cause is why it happens.
2. Why should more than one solution be considered? **Answer:** Different options may have different costs, risks and results.
3. What evidence can show that a solution worked? **Answer:** Measured results compared with the original problem.

## Lesson 14 — Using resources economically and responsibly
**Indicator:** CAREER-M2-03 — มีจิตสำนึกในการทำงานและใช้ทรัพยากรในการปฏิบัติงานอย่างประหยัดและคุ้มค่า  
**Outcomes:** Use resources responsibly and evaluate waste in a work task.  
**Starter:** Compare two plans for producing the same classroom item with different material use.  
**Activity:** Prepare a resource budget for a small project and identify safe reuse opportunities.  
**Evidence:** Chooses a resource-efficient method and explains why.  
**Questions**
1. What does value-for-resource use mean? **Answer:** Getting useful results while avoiding unnecessary consumption or waste.
2. Why should safety not be sacrificed to save resources? **Answer:** Preventing harm is more important than reducing cost.
3. Give one way to reduce waste in a project. **Answer:** Measure materials before cutting or making items.

## Lesson 15 — Building occupational experience
**Indicator:** CAREER-M2-04 — อธิบายการเสริมสร้างประสบการณ์อาชีพ  
**Outcomes:** Explain how projects, observation and practice build career understanding.  
**Starter:** Ask what a learner could discover by observing a workplace or completing a related school project.  
**Activity:** Design a safe, school-based career-experience activity with a learning goal and reflection.  
**Evidence:** Explains what experience can teach about a job.  
**Questions**
1. What is occupational experience? **Answer:** Learning about work through observation, practice or related activities.
2. Why should experience be connected to a learning goal? **Answer:** It makes the activity purposeful and easier to evaluate.
3. Name one safe way to explore a career at school. **Answer:** A supervised project or a talk by a volunteer professional.

## Lesson 16 — Preparing to enter an occupation
**Indicator:** CAREER-M2-05 — ระบุการเตรียมตัวเข้าสู่อาชีพ  
**Outcomes:** Identify relevant education, skills, experience and responsible preparation.  
**Starter:** Present a fictional job profile and ask what a candidate would need to prepare.  
**Activity:** Create a preparation pathway using verified entry requirements for a chosen occupation.  
**Evidence:** Identifies realistic preparation steps and information sources.  
**Questions**
1. Why should entry requirements be checked from reliable sources? **Answer:** Requirements can change and differ between occupations or institutions.
2. Name two ways to prepare for work. **Answer:** Develop relevant skills and gain suitable experience.
3. Why is communication useful in many jobs? **Answer:** It supports teamwork, customer service and clear instructions.

## Lesson 17 — Practising basic occupational skills
**Indicator:** CAREER-M2-06 — มีทักษะพื้นฐานที่จำเป็นสำหรับการประกอบอาชีพที่สนใจ  
**Outcomes:** Practise transferable skills such as teamwork, organisation, research and problem solving.  
**Starter:** Learners identify skills used in several different occupations.  
**Activity:** Complete a simulated workplace task with roles, instructions and a quality checklist.  
**Evidence:** Demonstrates one transferable skill and reflects on improvement.  
**Questions**
1. What is a transferable skill? **Answer:** A skill useful across different tasks or occupations.
2. Give one example of a workplace problem-solving skill. **Answer:** Identifying the cause and testing a suitable solution.
3. Why is feedback useful when practising a skill? **Answer:** It shows what is working and what needs improvement.

# M3 — Efficient work and career decision-making

## Lesson 18 — Discussing efficient work steps
**Indicator:** CAREER-M3-01 — อภิปรายขั้นตอนการทำงานที่มีประสิทธิภาพ  
**Outcomes:** Analyse work steps, sequence tasks and identify inefficiency.  
**Starter:** Compare two workflows for completing the same task.  
**Activity:** Map a workflow and recommend improvements in sequence, time or resource use.  
**Evidence:** Uses reasons and evidence to explain an efficient process.  
**Questions**
1. What makes a work process efficient? **Answer:** It achieves the goal with appropriate time, effort and resources while maintaining quality and safety.
2. Why should quality be considered alongside speed? **Answer:** Fast work is not useful if it is unsafe or poor quality.
3. How can a workflow be improved? **Answer:** Remove unnecessary steps, clarify roles and prepare resources.

## Lesson 19 — Teamwork with ethical conduct
**Indicator:** CAREER-M3-02 — ใช้ทักษะในการทำงานร่วมกันอย่างมีคุณธรรม  
**Outcomes:** Apply fairness, honesty, respect and accountability in teamwork.  
**Starter:** Discuss a fictional case where a team member takes credit for another person's work.  
**Activity:** Teams complete a project with a contribution log and peer reflection.  
**Evidence:** Shares credit fairly and fulfils agreed responsibilities.  
**Questions**
1. Why should contributions be acknowledged honestly? **Answer:** It is fair and builds trust.
2. What should a team do if work is distributed unfairly? **Answer:** Discuss the issue and agree on a fair redistribution.
3. Give one example of ethical teamwork. **Answer:** Reporting progress truthfully and respecting others' contributions.

## Lesson 20 — Managing work sustainably
**Indicator:** CAREER-M3-03 — อภิปรายการทำงานโดยใช้ทักษะการจัดการเพื่อการประหยัดพลังงาน ทรัพยากร และสิ่งแวดล้อม  
**Outcomes:** Plan work while considering energy, resources and environmental impact.  
**Starter:** Compare two production plans and identify their resource impacts.  
**Activity:** Complete a simple environmental impact checklist for a school project.  
**Evidence:** Recommends realistic ways to reduce resource use and environmental harm.  
**Questions**
1. What should be considered when planning sustainable work? **Answer:** Resource use, energy, waste, safety and environmental effects.
2. Why is the cheapest method not always the best? **Answer:** It may create greater waste, risk or long-term cost.
3. Give one way to reduce the environmental impact of a project. **Answer:** Choose durable materials and plan to minimise waste.

## Lesson 21 — Exploring different job-search methods
**Indicator:** CAREER-M3-04 — อภิปรายการหางานด้วยวิธีที่หลากหลาย  
**Outcomes:** Identify job-search channels and evaluate their reliability and suitability.  
**Starter:** List possible ways people learn about job opportunities.  
**Activity:** Compare a public employment service, company career page and verified job platform using a source-checking rubric.  
**Evidence:** Identifies multiple methods and checks credibility.  
**Questions**
1. Name two ways to search for work. **Answer:** Official employment services and company career pages.
2. What should be checked before responding to a job advertisement? **Answer:** Employer identity, role details, requirements and signs of scams.
3. Why should applicants protect personal information? **Answer:** To reduce privacy and fraud risks.

## Lesson 22 — Analysing pathways into occupations
**Indicator:** CAREER-M3-05 — วิเคราะห์แนวทางเข้าสู่อาชีพ  
**Outcomes:** Analyse qualifications, training, experience and progression routes.  
**Starter:** Give a fictional occupation profile and ask learners to map a possible pathway.  
**Activity:** Create a pathway diagram using current, verified requirements from official sources.  
**Evidence:** Shows logical steps and identifies where further information is needed.  
**Questions**
1. What is a career pathway? **Answer:** A sequence of learning, training and experience that can lead toward an occupation.
2. Why can pathways differ for the same occupation? **Answer:** Requirements and routes may vary by employer, location or specialisation.
3. What should be done if information is uncertain? **Answer:** Verify it with an official or qualified source.

## Lesson 23 — Evaluating career options
**Indicator:** CAREER-M3-06 — ประเมินทางเลือกในการประกอบอาชีพที่สอดคล้องกับความรู้ ความถนัดและความสนใจของตนเอง  
**Outcomes:** Compare career options against interests, abilities, values and realistic requirements.  
**Starter:** Compare two fictional career profiles with different tasks and qualifications.  
**Activity:** Use a decision matrix to evaluate options; learners keep personal results private if they wish.  
**Evidence:** Justifies a choice using relevant criteria rather than popularity alone.  
**Questions**
1. Name three criteria for evaluating a career option. **Answer:** Interests, abilities and required qualifications; values and work conditions also matter.
2. Why should a decision matrix use clear criteria? **Answer:** It makes comparisons more consistent and transparent.
3. Should one activity decide a person's whole future career? **Answer:** No. Interests and opportunities can develop over time.

# M4–M6 — Work, enterprise and career readiness

**Evidence:** Connects methods, skills and ethical responsibilities.

**Questions**
1. What is a prototype? **Answer:** An early model used to test an idea.

**Evidence:** Produces a workable plan and explains adjustments.  
**Questions**

3. What should a project manager do when progress falls behind? **Answer:** Identify the cause and revise the plan responsibly.

## Lesson 27 — Solving problems in work
**Indicator:** CAREER-M46-04 — มีทักษะกระบวนการแก้ปัญหาในการทำงาน

## Lesson 28 — Seeking knowledge for lifelong living

**Evidence:** Uses credible sources and distinguishes fact from opinion.  
**Questions**
1. What makes an information source credible? **Answer:** Clear authorship, evidence, accuracy and appropriate expertise.
2. Why should information be cross-checked? **Answer:** To reduce errors and identify conflicting claims.
3. How can new knowledge be applied responsibly? **Answer:** Check relevance and safety before using it in practice.

## Lesson 29 — Ethics and work habits

**Outcomes:** Demonstrate honesty, responsibility, perseverance and disciplined work habits.

**Activity:** Write a code of conduct for a simulated project team and evaluate scenarios against it.  
**Evidence:** Explains ethical choices and demonstrates responsible conduct.  
**Questions**

2. Why is punctuality important? **Answer:** It supports coordination and respects other people's time.
3. How can perseverance be balanced with safety? **Answer:** Continue responsibly but stop and seek help when a task is unsafe.

2. Why should a product's whole lifecycle be considered? **Answer:** Impacts occur during production, use and disposal.
