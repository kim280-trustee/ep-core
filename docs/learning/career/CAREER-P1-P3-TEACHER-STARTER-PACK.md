# Thai National Curriculum — Career Education (การงานอาชีพ)
## P1–P3 Teacher Starter Pack

**Curriculum:** Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), with applicable revisions  
**Learning area:** Occupations/Career (การงานอาชีพ)  
**Coverage in this pack:** P1–P3, standard ง 1.1  
**Database objective codes:** CAREER-P1-01 through CAREER-P3-03  
**Status:** Original teacher support material mapped to the registered indicators; not a replacement for OBEC curriculum documents or school-approved local curriculum.

## Teacher guidance

Teach through demonstration, guided practice and real classroom/home routines. Check tools before use, supervise cutting or other potentially hazardous activities, and adapt tasks to students' physical abilities and family circumstances. Do not require learners to bring household materials or disclose private family circumstances. Assess the process (planning, safe tool use, care, cooperation and reflection), not only the finished product.

## P1 — Working to help oneself

### Lesson 1 — Understanding work that helps me
**Indicator:** CAREER-P1-01 — บอกวิธีการทำงานเพื่อช่วยเหลือตนเอง  
**Learning outcomes:** Name simple jobs that help oneself; explain the steps in one familiar task; identify when help from an adult is needed.  
**Starter:** Show pictures of a school bag, shoes, a lunch tray and a plant. Ask: “Which jobs can you do by yourself?”  
**Teaching sequence:** Model one routine using three steps: prepare, do, put away. Learners sequence picture cards for packing a school bag or tidying a desk.  
**Practical task:** Learners demonstrate one safe self-help routine and explain its steps.  
**Assessment evidence:** Correct sequence and a clear verbal explanation.  
**Practice**
1. Name one job you can do to help yourself. **Answer:** Example: pack my school bag.
2. What should you do before starting a task? **Answer:** Prepare the materials and check that the place is safe.
3. Why should you put materials away after working? **Answer:** To keep them safe, clean and easy to find.

### Lesson 2 — Using simple tools safely
**Indicator:** CAREER-P1-02 — ใช้วัสดุ อุปกรณ์ และเครื่องมือง่าย ๆ ในการทำงานอย่างปลอดภัย  
**Learning outcomes:** Identify common classroom tools; choose a suitable tool for a simple task; follow safe handling and storage rules.  
**Starter:** Display a pencil, ruler, glue stick and child-safe scissors. Ask learners to match each tool to a job.  
**Teaching sequence:** Demonstrate carrying, sharing, using and returning tools safely. Discuss asking for help when a tool is unfamiliar or damaged.  
**Practical task:** Complete a supervised paper-folding or collage activity using appropriate tools.  
**Assessment evidence:** Selects an appropriate tool, follows safety directions and returns it correctly.  
**Practice**
1. Which tool can you use to measure a straight line? **Answer:** A ruler.
2. What should you do if classroom scissors are damaged? **Answer:** Stop using them and tell the teacher.
3. Where should tools be placed after use? **Answer:** In their correct storage place.

### Lesson 3 — Working actively and on time
**Indicator:** CAREER-P1-03 — ทำงานเพื่อช่วยเหลือตนเองอย่างกระตือรือร้นและตรงเวลา  
**Learning outcomes:** Begin a familiar task when asked; complete it carefully; finish within an agreed time.  
**Starter:** Give learners two picture schedules and ask which one shows a ready-to-learn routine.  
**Teaching sequence:** Model listening to instructions, beginning promptly, checking work and tidying up.  
**Practical task:** Learners organise their desk and learning materials within a short, supportive time window.  
**Assessment evidence:** Starts promptly, completes the agreed task and leaves the area tidy.  
**Practice**
1. What does “on time” mean? **Answer:** Doing something at the agreed time.
2. What should you do after listening to the task instructions? **Answer:** Begin the task carefully.
3. What can you do if you cannot finish a task? **Answer:** Tell the teacher and ask what to do next.

## P2 — Working to help oneself and family

### Lesson 4 — Jobs and their benefits
**Indicator:** CAREER-P2-01 — บอกวิธีการและประโยชน์การทำงานเพื่อช่วยเหลือตนเองและครอบครัว  
**Learning outcomes:** Describe simple tasks that support daily life; explain how shared responsibilities help a household; recognise that tasks differ between families.  
**Starter:** Ask learners to identify ways people keep shared spaces clean and organised.  
**Teaching sequence:** Discuss age-appropriate tasks without assuming every household has the same roles. Model a sequence for organising personal belongings.  
**Practical task:** Create a “responsibility choice” chart with one safe task a learner can practise.  
**Assessment evidence:** Explains a task and one benefit, such as cleanliness, safety or saving time.  
**Practice**
1. Give one example of a task that helps a household. **Answer:** Example: putting away one's own belongings.
2. How can sharing suitable tasks help people at home? **Answer:** It helps keep the home organised and reduces the work for one person.
3. Do all families have exactly the same jobs? **Answer:** No. Jobs depend on each family's needs and circumstances.

### Lesson 5 — Choosing materials and tools economically
**Indicator:** CAREER-P2-02 — ใช้วัสดุ อุปกรณ์ และเครื่องมือในการทำงานอย่างเหมาะสมกับงานและประหยัด  
**Learning outcomes:** Match materials and tools to a task; avoid unnecessary waste; use and store materials carefully.  
**Starter:** Show two ways to wrap a book and ask which uses materials more carefully.  
**Teaching sequence:** Compare suitable materials, estimate what is needed and model careful use of supplies.  
**Practical task:** Make a simple useful item from clean reusable classroom materials.  
**Assessment evidence:** Chooses suitable materials and avoids unnecessary waste.  
**Practice**
1. Why should you choose materials before starting? **Answer:** To make sure they are suitable and enough for the task.
2. What does using materials economically mean? **Answer:** Using only what is needed and avoiding waste.
3. What should you do with reusable leftover materials? **Answer:** Keep them safely for another suitable task.

### Lesson 6 — Safe work for oneself and family
**Indicator:** CAREER-P2-03 — ทำงานเพื่อช่วยเหลือตนเองและครอบครัวอย่างปลอดภัย  
**Learning outcomes:** Recognise common hazards; follow a safe sequence; know when to ask an adult for help.  
**Starter:** Show safe/unsafe picture pairs of a tidy floor, spilled water, electrical sockets and cleaning products.  
**Teaching sequence:** Identify hazards, stop unsafe actions, inform an adult and clean only when appropriate and supervised.  
**Practical task:** Conduct a classroom safety walk and suggest one improvement.  
**Assessment evidence:** Identifies hazards and explains an appropriate safe response.  
**Practice**
1. What should you do when you see water spilled on a walkway? **Answer:** Warn others and tell an adult; clean it only if it is safe and permitted.
2. Should children handle unknown cleaning chemicals? **Answer:** No. They should leave them alone and tell an adult.
3. What is the first priority when doing a job? **Answer:** Safety.

## P3 — Working systematically and responsibly

### Lesson 7 — Work that benefits self, family and community
**Indicator:** CAREER-P3-01 — อธิบายวิธีการและประโยชน์การทำงานเพื่อช่วยเหลือตนเอง ครอบครัว และส่วนรวม  
**Learning outcomes:** Explain how work supports different groups; describe a simple work plan; recognise responsibility to shared spaces.  
**Starter:** Ask: “Who benefits when our classroom is clean?”  
**Teaching sequence:** Compare a personal task, a shared classroom task and a community task. Identify beneficiaries and steps.  
**Practical task:** Plan and carry out a short classroom improvement activity in teams.  
**Assessment evidence:** Explains the task, its beneficiaries and the steps taken.  
**Practice**
1. Give one example of work that benefits the community. **Answer:** Helping keep a shared public area clean.
2. Why is planning useful before group work? **Answer:** It helps people understand the steps and share responsibilities.
3. Who benefits from keeping a classroom tidy? **Answer:** Everyone who uses the classroom.

### Lesson 8 — Selecting tools for the job
**Indicator:** CAREER-P3-02 — ใช้วัสดุ อุปกรณ์ และเครื่องมือตรงกับลักษณะงาน  
**Learning outcomes:** Identify the requirements of a task; select appropriate tools; explain why a tool is suitable.  
**Starter:** Match pictures of a watering can, broom, ruler and glue to different jobs.  
**Teaching sequence:** Model checking the task, selecting tools, checking condition and using them correctly.  
**Practical task:** In supervised stations, learners select tools for organising books, watering plants or making a paper item.  
**Assessment evidence:** Correct tool selection and a reason for the choice.  
**Practice**
1. Which tool is suitable for watering a small plant? **Answer:** A watering can or suitable small water container.
2. Why is a ruler useful when measuring paper? **Answer:** It helps measure a straight length accurately.
3. What should you check before using a tool? **Answer:** That it is suitable, safe and in good condition.

### Lesson 9 — Following a work process carefully
**Indicator:** CAREER-P3-03 — ทำงานอย่างเป็นขั้นตอนตามกระบวนการทำงาน ด้วยความสะอาด ความรอบคอบ และอนุรักษ์สิ่งแวดล้อม  
**Learning outcomes:** Plan, perform and review a task in order; maintain cleanliness; use resources responsibly.  
**Starter:** Present mixed-up picture cards for preparing and cleaning a work area. Learners put them in a sensible order.  
**Teaching sequence:** Teach a simple cycle: plan → prepare → do → check → clean and store. Discuss reducing waste and caring for shared resources.  
**Practical task:** Complete a small classroom organisation or recycled-material craft project using the work cycle.  
**Assessment evidence:** Follows the sequence, works carefully, keeps the area clean and minimises waste.  
**Practice**
1. What are the main stages of a work process? **Answer:** Plan, prepare, do, check and tidy/store.
2. Why should you check your work before finishing? **Answer:** To find mistakes and make sure the task is complete and safe.
3. Give one way to protect the environment while working. **Answer:** Reuse suitable materials or avoid wasting water and supplies.

## Simple observation rubric

| Criterion | 2 — Consistent | 1 — With support | 0 — Not yet |
|---|---|---|---|
| Planning | Follows the task sequence | Needs reminders | Cannot yet sequence the task |
| Tool choice and safety | Chooses and uses tools safely | Needs safety reminders | Uses tools unsafely or chooses unsuitable tools |
| Work habits | Works carefully and completes the task | Completes with frequent prompts | Does not yet complete the task |
| Care for resources | Keeps area clean and avoids waste | Needs reminders | Does not yet care for materials/work area |

**Suggested use:** Record observation during practical work; use the three practice questions as short oral or written checks. Adapt language and task complexity to the learners' reading level.
