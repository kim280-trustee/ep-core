# Thai National Curriculum — Career Education (การงานอาชีพ)
## P4–P5 Teacher Starter Pack

**Curriculum:** Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E.2551 (2008), applicable revisions  
**Learning area:** Occupations/Career (การงานอาชีพ)  
**Coverage:** P4 (5 indicators) and P5 (6 indicators)  
**Status:** Original teacher-created lessons aligned to the registered indicator statements. This is a teaching resource, not a substitute for the official curriculum.

## Teacher guidance
Use locally familiar, safe examples such as organising classroom materials, caring for plants, reducing waste and learning about community occupations. Do not assume that every learner has the same household responsibilities or access to the same resources. For occupation research, obtain permission before interviewing anyone and do not collect private personal or financial information.

## P4 — Work processes, responsibility and occupations

### Lesson 1 — Why plan work toward a goal?
**Indicator:** CAREER-P4-01 — อธิบายเหตุผลในการทำงานให้บรรลุเป้าหมาย  
**Outcomes:** Explain why a task needs a goal; identify the resources and steps needed; describe how checking progress helps.  
**Starter:** Show a messy classroom shelf and a neatly organised shelf. Ask what goal the class could set.  
**Teach:** Model a simple plan: goal, materials, steps, time and check.  
**Practical task:** Teams plan how to organise one classroom area.  
**Evidence:** Learner explains the goal and why the selected steps help achieve it.  
**Questions**
1. Why should a task have a clear goal? **Answer:** It shows what needs to be achieved and helps guide the work.
2. What should a plan identify before work begins? **Answer:** The goal, materials, steps and time.
3. How can you tell whether the goal has been achieved? **Answer:** Compare the finished result with the goal or checklist.

### Lesson 2 — Completing a planned task responsibly
**Indicator:** CAREER-P4-02 — ทำงานบรรลุเป้าหมายที่วางไว้อย่างเป็นขั้นตอนด้วยความขยัน อดทน รับผิดชอบ และซื่อสัตย์  
**Outcomes:** Follow a sequence; persist when a task is difficult; take responsibility and report work honestly.  
**Starter:** Present a simple task with three steps and ask what might happen if the middle step is skipped.  
**Teach:** Demonstrate following a plan, checking quality and reporting what was completed.  
**Practical task:** Learners complete a short classroom organisation task using a checklist.  
**Evidence:** Completes steps, uses time responsibly and reports progress truthfully.  
**Questions**
1. What does responsibility mean during a group task? **Answer:** Completing your agreed part and caring for shared materials.
2. What should you do when a step is difficult? **Answer:** Try a suitable solution, persist and ask for help when needed.
3. Why is it important to report unfinished work honestly? **Answer:** So the group can understand the real progress and plan the next step.

### Lesson 3 — Good manners while working
**Indicator:** CAREER-P4-03 — ปฏิบัติตนอย่างมีมารยาทในการทำงาน  
**Outcomes:** Listen respectfully; take turns; share tools; give helpful feedback.  
**Starter:** Compare two short role-plays: interrupting a teammate and listening before responding.  
**Teach:** Practise polite requests, turn-taking and disagreement without insults.  
**Practical task:** Complete a paired sorting or construction activity, observing respectful teamwork.  
**Evidence:** Uses respectful language and cooperates appropriately.  
**Questions**
1. What can you say when you need to borrow a tool? **Answer:** “May I borrow the ruler, please?”
2. What should you do when a teammate is speaking? **Answer:** Listen and wait for your turn.
3. How can you disagree politely? **Answer:** Explain your reason calmly and listen to the other person's view.

### Lesson 4 — Saving energy and resources
**Indicator:** CAREER-P4-04 — ใช้พลังงานและทรัพยากรในการทำงานอย่างประหยัดและคุ้มค่า  
**Outcomes:** Identify resources used in a task; reduce unnecessary use; reuse suitable materials safely.  
**Starter:** Show examples of lights left on in an empty room and paper used on both sides.  
**Teach:** Discuss electricity, water, paper and classroom supplies; distinguish safe reuse from unsafe reuse.  
**Practical task:** Carry out a classroom resource check and propose two realistic improvements.  
**Evidence:** Identifies avoidable waste and demonstrates a practical saving action.  
**Questions**
1. Give one way to save electricity in a classroom. **Answer:** Switch off lights and fans when they are not needed and it is safe to do so.
2. Why should we plan material quantities before a task? **Answer:** To avoid taking more than we need.
3. Give one example of reusing a resource. **Answer:** Use clean one-sided paper for drafts or practice.

### Lesson 5 — Meaning and importance of occupations
**Indicator:** CAREER-P4-05 — อธิบายความหมายและความสำคัญของอาชีพ  
**Outcomes:** Explain what an occupation is; identify how occupations meet community needs; recognise that occupations require different skills.  
**Starter:** Display pictures of a farmer, nurse, mechanic and shopkeeper. Ask what each contributes.  
**Teach:** Define occupation as work people do to earn a living or provide a service; discuss the value of different kinds of work.  
**Practical task:** Learners create a community occupations map showing services and goods people provide.  
**Evidence:** Explains the meaning of occupation and one contribution to society.  
**Questions**
1. What is an occupation? **Answer:** Work a person does, commonly to earn a living or provide a service.
2. Why are different occupations important in a community? **Answer:** They provide different goods and services people need.
3. Name one skill a mechanic may need. **Answer:** Problem-solving and practical repair skills.

## P5 — Work management, resource responsibility and career awareness

### Lesson 6 — Explaining each step in a work process
**Indicator:** CAREER-P5-01 — อธิบายเหตุผลในการทำงานแต่ละขั้นตอนถูกต้องตามกระบวนการทำงาน  
**Outcomes:** Sequence a task; explain why each step is needed; identify where checking should happen.  
**Starter:** Give learners mixed-up picture cards for preparing a class display.  
**Teach:** Use the sequence: analyse task, plan, prepare, perform, check and improve.  
**Practical task:** Groups prepare a step-by-step plan for a simple class display or plant-care task.  
**Evidence:** Sequences steps and gives a reason for each key step.  
**Questions**
1. Why should you analyse a task before doing it? **Answer:** To understand what is required and what resources are needed.
2. Why is checking part of the work process? **Answer:** It helps identify errors and improve the result.
3. What might happen if preparation is skipped? **Answer:** Materials may be missing and work may be delayed or unsafe.

### Lesson 7 — Managing work systematically and creatively
**Indicator:** CAREER-P5-02 — ใช้ทักษะการจัดการในการทำงานอย่างเป็นระบบ ประณีต และมีความคิดสร้างสรรค์  
**Outcomes:** Organise time and materials; divide responsibilities; produce careful work and consider improvements.  
**Starter:** Compare an unlabelled pile of materials with an organised work station.  
**Teach:** Model sorting, prioritising, assigning roles and checking quality. Encourage multiple valid solutions.  
**Practical task:** Teams design and make a useful organiser from clean reusable materials.  
**Evidence:** Work area is organised, roles are clear and product is carefully made.  
**Questions**
1. What does managing work systematically involve? **Answer:** Planning, organising resources, arranging steps and checking progress.
2. Why should a group divide responsibilities? **Answer:** It helps everyone contribute and prevents important tasks being missed.
3. How can creativity improve a task? **Answer:** It can produce a useful or more effective solution.

### Lesson 8 — Working with family members respectfully
**Indicator:** CAREER-P5-03 — ปฏิบัติตนอย่างมีมารยาทในการทำงานกับสมาชิกในครอบครัว  
**Outcomes:** Communicate politely; agree on age-appropriate responsibilities; respect different views and needs.  
**Starter:** Read a scenario about two people sharing a simple organising task. Ask how they can agree fairly.  
**Teach:** Discuss listening, asking before using belongings, thanking others and seeking adult guidance for unsafe tasks.  
**Practical task:** Learners create a fictional shared-task plan that shows respectful communication.  
**Evidence:** Suggests polite language and fair cooperation without assuming a particular family structure.  
**Questions**
1. What should you do before using another person's belongings? **Answer:** Ask permission.
2. How can you respond if someone suggests a different way to do a task? **Answer:** Listen, discuss the options politely and agree on a suitable approach.
3. Why should responsibilities be suitable for the person's age and ability? **Answer:** To keep the task fair and safe.

### Lesson 9 — Using energy and resources wisely
**Indicator:** CAREER-P5-04 — มีจิตสำนึกในการใช้พลังงานและทรัพยากรอย่างประหยัดและคุ้มค่า  
**Outcomes:** Recognise resource impacts; practise careful consumption; propose sustainable improvements.  
**Starter:** Ask learners to identify resources used to make a paper notebook.  
**Teach:** Trace resource use and discuss reduce, reuse and recycle, with safety and local recycling rules.  
**Practical task:** Conduct a paper-use audit in class and agree on a waste-reduction action.  
**Evidence:** Gives a reasoned proposal and demonstrates resource-conscious behaviour.  
**Questions**
1. What is one difference between reducing and reusing? **Answer:** Reducing means using less; reusing means using an item again.
2. Why should recycling rules be checked locally? **Answer:** Accepted materials and sorting methods can differ by location.
3. Give one practical way to reduce classroom waste. **Answer:** Plan printing carefully and use suitable paper on both sides.

### Lesson 10 — Exploring occupations in the community
**Indicator:** CAREER-P5-05 — สำรวจข้อมูลที่เกี่ยวกับอาชีพต่าง ๆ ในชุมชน  
**Outcomes:** Identify reliable ways to learn about occupations; prepare respectful questions; organise findings.  
**Starter:** Ask learners what they would like to know about a local occupation.  
**Teach:** Model a short interview plan. Emphasise permission, respectful questions and avoiding private information.  
**Practical task:** Use teacher-provided profiles or conduct an approved interview with a volunteer to record job tasks, tools and skills.  
**Evidence:** Records relevant information from an appropriate source.  
**Questions**
1. Name two safe sources of information about occupations. **Answer:** Teacher-provided profiles and approved interviews with volunteers.
2. Give one respectful question for an interview. **Answer:** “What skills are useful in your work?”
3. Why should you ask permission before an interview? **Answer:** To respect the person's choice and privacy.

### Lesson 11 — Comparing different occupations
**Indicator:** CAREER-P5-06 — ระบุความแตกต่างของอาชีพ  
**Outcomes:** Compare tasks, tools, skills and work settings; recognise that occupations have different contributions.  
**Starter:** Compare a farmer and a nurse: what tasks and tools might differ?  
**Teach:** Build a comparison chart using work tasks, tools, knowledge, skills and service provided.  
**Practical task:** Learners compare two occupations using teacher-approved information.  
**Evidence:** Identifies at least two accurate differences and one contribution from each occupation.  
**Questions**
1. Name two features that can be used to compare occupations. **Answer:** Work tasks and tools; other examples include skills and work settings.
2. Why might a farmer and a nurse use different tools? **Answer:** Their work tasks and purposes are different.
3. Does one occupation make every other occupation unimportant? **Answer:** No. Different occupations meet different community needs.

## Observation rubric

| Criterion | 2 — Consistent | 1 — With support | 0 — Not yet |
|---|---|---|---|
| Planning and sequencing | Plans and explains steps | Needs prompts | Cannot yet sequence steps |
| Responsibility and teamwork | Completes role and communicates respectfully | Needs reminders | Does not yet contribute appropriately |
| Resource use | Uses materials carefully and avoids waste | Needs reminders | Wastes or misuses resources |
| Career understanding | Explains occupations and compares accurately | Partial explanation | Needs further teaching |

**Assessment:** Use the questions for short formative checks and the rubric during practical tasks. Adjust reading and writing demands to the learners' level.

## Alignment and source note
The Thai indicator statements above are the curriculum alignment targets. Lesson explanations, practical activities, examples, rubric and questions are original teacher-created materials. Consult the official OBEC curriculum documents and school-approved curriculum when planning formal assessment.
