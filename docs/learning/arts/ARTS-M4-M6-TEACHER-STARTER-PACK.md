# Arts (ศิลปะ) — M4–M6 Teacher Starter Pack

**Curriculum:** Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551, OBEC 2021 must-know/should-know indicator guide.  
**Scope:** 39 shared upper-secondary indicators, each aligned to M4, M5 and M6.  
**Classification:** 27 must-know; 12 should-know.  
**Format:** One 50-minute lesson per indicator; three original short-response/practical assessment prompts per lesson (117 prompts total). The answer guidance is a marking rubric, not a single memorised answer.

## Teaching routine (50 minutes)
- 5 min — visual, audio or performance hook; activate prior knowledge.
- 10 min — teacher modelling and key vocabulary.
- 15 min — guided analysis or demonstration.
- 15 min — student creation, rehearsal, comparison or presentation.
- 5 min — exit response and evidence check.

## Assessment guidance
Award credit for accurate subject vocabulary, evidence from the artwork/music/performance, a reasoned explanation, and appropriate practical application. For practical tasks, assess process and reflection as well as the final product.

## Lesson 01 — วิเคราะห์การใช้ทัศนธาตุและหลักการออกแบบในการสื่อความหมายในรูปแบบต่าง ๆ

**Objective:** `ARTS-M46-01` — วิเคราะห์การใช้ทัศนธาตุและหลักการออกแบบในการสื่อความหมายในรูปแบบต่าง ๆ

**Arts discipline:** Visual arts
**Grade band:** Upper secondary (M4–M6)
**Indicator focus:** line, colour, shape, form, texture, space and the design choice named in the indicator

- **Objective code:** `ARTS-M46-01`
- **OBEC standard:** ศ 1.1
- **Classification:** Must-know
- **Learning intention:** Students can วิเคราะห์การใช้ทัศนธาตุและหลักการออกแบบในการสื่อความหมายในรูปแบบต่าง ๆ.
- **Key content:** วิเคราะห์การใช้ทัศนธาตุและหลักการออกแบบในการสื่อความหมายในรูปแบบต่าง ๆ. Connect the concept to Thai cultural examples and relevant contemporary practice.
- **Teacher input:** Introduce the vocabulary and model one worked example. Use a local artwork, Thai musical example, or Thai performance where appropriate, then compare with an international example.
- **Guided practice:** เลือกงานศิลปะตัวอย่าง 2 ชิ้น วิเคราะห์องค์ประกอบที่เห็น อภิปรายการเลือกใช้วัสดุ/เทคนิค และร่างหรือสร้างผลงานสั้น ๆ ที่นำหลักการไปใช้
- **Independent evidence:** Each student submits a short analysis and a practical response, sketch, performance excerpt, or plan appropriate to the topic.

**Practical activity:** Use two contrasting artworks or visual examples that make line, colour, shape, form, texture, space and the design choice named in the indicator visible. Model how to inspect an example, name the feature and point to evidence. Learners complete a focused studio or analysis task directly addressing “วิเคราะห์การใช้ทัศนธาตุและหลักการออกแบบในการสื่อความหมายในรูปแบบต่าง ๆ”. Use paper, pencils/colouring tools and safe recycled or modelling materials as needed; demonstrate scissors, adhesives and cutting tools, provide non-toxic materials and keep workspaces clear. For upper secondary learners, plan independently, critically compare alternatives and justify decisions with evidence. Evaluate context, constraints and audience, refine against explicit criteria and defend choices.

**Expected evidence:** Collect an annotated example and an indicator-matched product: sketch, model, print test, graphic, comparison board or critique sheet. Evidence must show at least two labelled decisions linked to line, colour, shape, form, texture, space and the design choice named in the indicator, one visible feature that meets “วิเคราะห์การใช้ทัศนธาตุและหลักการออกแบบในการสื่อความหมายในรูปแบบต่าง ๆ”, and a brief explanation or revision note. Accept an oral explanation with teacher checklist when writing is not the target skill.

**Teacher check:** Use a three-point rubric: (1) accurate identification or explanation of line, colour, shape, form, texture, space and the design choice named in the indicator; (2) visible, purposeful use or analysis of the required visual feature; (3) evidence-based explanation or one feasible improvement. Assess against “วิเคราะห์การใช้ทัศนธาตุและหลักการออกแบบในการสื่อความหมายในรูปแบบต่าง ๆ”, not personal taste, neatness alone or access to expensive materials. Accept safe, low-cost substitutions and culturally respectful interpretations.

**Independent practice**
1. **Knowledge / close observation:** Using the supplied artwork or visual sample, identify two specific features that demonstrate “วิเคราะห์การใช้ทัศนธาตุและหลักการออกแบบในการสื่อความหมายในรูปแบบต่าง ๆ”. For each, name the relevant part of line, colour, shape, form, texture, space and the design choice named in the indicator and point to the exact visual, audible or performed evidence. **Answer:** Identify two accurate, distinct features; use subject vocabulary; cite evidence for each; connect both features to the indicator rather than offering unsupported opinion.
2. **Application / fictional case:** In a fictional school exhibition design team, learners must demonstrate “วิเคราะห์การใช้ทัศนธาตุและหลักการออกแบบในการสื่อความหมายในรูปแบบต่าง ๆ”. Explain a three-step plan: identify the challenge, apply line, colour, shape, form, texture, space and the design choice named in the indicator to choose a response, and check whether the result meets the indicator. **Answer:** Give three workable steps in order, make an explicit discipline-specific decision, and justify how observable evidence will show that the indicator has been met.
3. **Demonstration / evidence product:** Produce an annotated study or small visual composition that demonstrates “วิเคราะห์การใช้ทัศนธาตุและหลักการออกแบบในการสื่อความหมายในรูปแบบต่าง ๆ”. Label or identify two deliberate choices connected to line, colour, shape, form, texture, space and the design choice named in the indicator, then explain how the evidence meets the indicator and what you would refine next. **Answer:** Submit the named product or performance evidence; show two indicator-linked choices; meet the observable criteria in the teacher check; explain one specific strength and one realistic next step.

**Marking (3 points):** 1 point for accurate indicator knowledge or interpretation; 1 point for applying the named discipline skill in the fictional case; 1 point for observable product/performance evidence and a justified reflection. Use the grade-band guidance above; accept accessible response modes that assess the same indicator.
