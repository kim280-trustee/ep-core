# Arts (ศิลปะ) — Primary 1–3 Teacher Starter Pack

**Curriculum:** Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551; OBEC 2021 indicator and must-know/should-know guide.  
**Coverage:** P1 18 indicators; P2 25; P3 29. **72 lessons and 216 original assessment prompts.**  
**Lesson duration:** 50 minutes. Adjust practical materials to school availability and local culture.

## Shared 50-minute lesson structure
- 5 min — show an image, object, sound or short performance; ask learners what they notice.
- 8 min — introduce the target idea and 2–4 simple subject words.
- 10 min — teacher demonstrates the skill or models how to observe/listen.
- 17 min — guided making, listening, movement or rehearsal.
- 7 min — learners share work or perform in pairs/groups.
- 3 min — exit check: one thing learned and one piece of evidence.

## Assessment approach
Use observation, short oral answers, drawings, practical performance and simple learner reflection. For young learners, accept pointing, matching, demonstrating and short spoken responses as evidence. Do not grade artistic taste; assess the target skill, participation, safe tool use and ability to explain a choice.

# P1 — Lesson sequence

## 1. อภิปรายเกี่ยวกับรูปร่าง ลักษณะ และขนาดของสิ่งต่าง ๆ รอบตัวในธรรมชาติและสิ่งที่มนุษย์สร้างขึ้น

**Objective:** `ARTS-P1-01` — อภิปรายเกี่ยวกับรูปร่าง ลักษณะ และขนาดของสิ่งต่าง ๆ รอบตัวในธรรมชาติและสิ่งที่มนุษย์สร้างขึ้น

**Arts discipline:** Visual arts
**Grade band:** Early primary (P1–P3)
**Indicator focus:** line, colour, shape, form, texture, space and the design choice named in the indicator

- **Objective:** `ARTS-P1-01`
- **OBEC standard:** ศ 1.1
- **Priority:** Must-know
- **Learning intention:** Learners can demonstrate: อภิปรายเกี่ยวกับรูปร่าง ลักษณะ และขนาดของสิ่งต่าง ๆ รอบตัวในธรรมชาติและสิ่งที่มนุษย์สร้างขึ้น.
- **Key teaching points:** Visual Arts. Explain the idea with concrete examples, model the action, and connect it to familiar Thai/local objects, songs or performances.
- **Teacher demonstration:** Show one clear example. Think aloud using simple language; model safe handling of materials, respectful listening or appropriate audience behaviour.
- **Guided activity:** Learners practise the skill with a partner or small group. For visual arts, use drawing, collage, modelling or printmaking materials; for music, use voice, body percussion or available instruments; for dance/drama, use safe movement space and simple gestures.
- **Independent evidence:** Each learner creates, identifies, performs or explains one example that directly demonstrates the indicator.

**Practical activity:** Use two contrasting artworks or visual examples that make line, colour, shape, form, texture, space and the design choice named in the indicator visible. Model how to inspect an example, name the feature and point to evidence. Learners complete a focused studio or analysis task directly addressing “อภิปรายเกี่ยวกับรูปร่าง ลักษณะ และขนาดของสิ่งต่าง ๆ รอบตัวในธรรมชาติและสิ่งที่มนุษย์สร้างขึ้น”. Use paper, pencils/colouring tools and safe recycled or modelling materials as needed; demonstrate scissors, adhesives and cutting tools, provide non-toxic materials and keep workspaces clear. For early primary learners, use short demonstrations, concrete pictures or objects, one step at a time and pointing, speaking, drawing or movement evidence. Keep tools and movement low-risk; model before pupils try.

**Expected evidence:** Collect an annotated example and an indicator-matched product: sketch, model, print test, graphic, comparison board or critique sheet. Evidence must show at least two labelled decisions linked to line, colour, shape, form, texture, space and the design choice named in the indicator, one visible feature that meets “อภิปรายเกี่ยวกับรูปร่าง ลักษณะ และขนาดของสิ่งต่าง ๆ รอบตัวในธรรมชาติและสิ่งที่มนุษย์สร้างขึ้น”, and a brief explanation or revision note. Accept an oral explanation with teacher checklist when writing is not the target skill.

**Teacher check:** Use a three-point rubric: (1) accurate identification or explanation of line, colour, shape, form, texture, space and the design choice named in the indicator; (2) visible, purposeful use or analysis of the required visual feature; (3) evidence-based explanation or one feasible improvement. Assess against “อภิปรายเกี่ยวกับรูปร่าง ลักษณะ และขนาดของสิ่งต่าง ๆ รอบตัวในธรรมชาติและสิ่งที่มนุษย์สร้างขึ้น”, not personal taste, neatness alone or access to expensive materials. Accept safe, low-cost substitutions and culturally respectful interpretations.

**Independent practice**
1. **Knowledge / close observation:** Using the supplied artwork or visual sample, identify two specific features that demonstrate “อภิปรายเกี่ยวกับรูปร่าง ลักษณะ และขนาดของสิ่งต่าง ๆ รอบตัวในธรรมชาติและสิ่งที่มนุษย์สร้างขึ้น”. For each, name the relevant part of line, colour, shape, form, texture, space and the design choice named in the indicator and point to the exact visual, audible or performed evidence. **Answer:** Identify two accurate, distinct features; use subject vocabulary; cite evidence for each; connect both features to the indicator rather than offering unsupported opinion.
2. **Application / fictional case:** In a fictional school exhibition design team, learners must demonstrate “อภิปรายเกี่ยวกับรูปร่าง ลักษณะ และขนาดของสิ่งต่าง ๆ รอบตัวในธรรมชาติและสิ่งที่มนุษย์สร้างขึ้น”. Explain a three-step plan: identify the challenge, apply line, colour, shape, form, texture, space and the design choice named in the indicator to choose a response, and check whether the result meets the indicator. **Answer:** Give three workable steps in order, make an explicit discipline-specific decision, and justify how observable evidence will show that the indicator has been met.
3. **Demonstration / evidence product:** Produce an annotated study or small visual composition that demonstrates “อภิปรายเกี่ยวกับรูปร่าง ลักษณะ และขนาดของสิ่งต่าง ๆ รอบตัวในธรรมชาติและสิ่งที่มนุษย์สร้างขึ้น”. Label or identify two deliberate choices connected to line, colour, shape, form, texture, space and the design choice named in the indicator, then explain how the evidence meets the indicator and what you would refine next. **Answer:** Submit the named product or performance evidence; show two indicator-linked choices; meet the observable criteria in the teacher check; explain one specific strength and one realistic next step.

**Marking (3 points):** 1 point for accurate indicator knowledge or interpretation; 1 point for applying the named discipline skill in the fictional case; 1 point for observable product/performance evidence and a justified reflection. Use the grade-band guidance above; accept accessible response modes that assess the same indicator.
