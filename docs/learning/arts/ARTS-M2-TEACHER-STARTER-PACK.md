# Arts M2 — Teacher Starter Pack

**Curriculum:** Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), OBEC must-know/should-know indicator guide.
**Grade:** M2 · **Official indicators:** 27 · **Lessons:** 27 · **Assessment prompts:** 81

Each lesson uses a 50-minute structure: 5 min warm-up/stimulus, 10 min explicit modelling, 20 min guided practice or creation, 10 min sharing/performance, 5 min exit assessment. Teachers should accept equivalent practical evidence where appropriate.

### Lesson 1: อภิปรายเกี่ยวกับทัศนธาตุในด้านรูปแบบและแนวคิดของงานทัศนศิลป์ที่เลือกมา

**Objective:** `ARTS-M2-01` — อภิปรายเกี่ยวกับทัศนธาตุในด้านรูปแบบและแนวคิดของงานทัศนศิลป์ที่เลือกมา

- **Official indicator:** ARTS-M2-01 (ศ 1.1) — must_know.
- **Learning outcome:** Students can demonstrate: อภิปรายเกี่ยวกับทัศนธาตุในด้านรูปแบบและแนวคิดของงานทัศนศิลป์ที่เลือกมา.
- **Materials:** Board, paper, pencils/colours, safe classroom materials, and relevant audio/visual examples.
- **Teaching sequence:** Introduce a familiar example; model the target skill; guide students through a short task; let students produce or perform independently; use peer feedback and an exit check.

**Practical activity:** Teach through an artwork, listening example or performance appropriate to visual-art elements and design. Model safe and respectful handling of materials, instruments, movement space and cultural content. Adapt to lower secondary learners using subject vocabulary and evidence-based analysis.

**Expected evidence:** Three completed tasks demonstrate ARTS-M2-01: accurate observation/listening, explanation or interpretation supported by evidence, and practical creation/performance or contextual response. Accept visual, oral, written, notation-based or performance evidence where it measures the same indicator.

**Teacher check:** Assess (1) accurate subject vocabulary, (2) evidence from the work, score or performance, and (3) intentional practical application or reasoned interpretation. Assess skill and process—not personal taste. Accept culturally respectful equivalent examples.

**Independent practice**
1. **Observe / identify:** Study an artwork or design related to “อภิปรายเกี่ยวกับทัศนธาตุในด้านรูปแบบและแนวคิดของงานทัศนศิลป์ที่เลือกมา”. Identify three relevant visual elements, materials, techniques, purposes or cultural features and point to evidence. **Answer:** Identify accurate features required by ARTS-M2-01, use suitable art vocabulary and point to visible evidence.
2. **Analyse / explain:** Annotate an example related to “อภิปรายเกี่ยวกับทัศนธาตุในด้านรูปแบบและแนวคิดของงานทัศนศิลป์ที่เลือกมา”. Explain how line, shape, colour, form, texture, space, balance, unity or technique communicates meaning. **Answer:** Connect an artistic choice to a visible effect or intended meaning with evidence.
3. **Create / improve:** Make a small artwork, model, graphic or design demonstrating “อภิปรายเกี่ยวกับทัศนธาตุในด้านรูปแบบและแนวคิดของงานทัศนศิลป์ที่เลือกมา”. Label your choices and identify one improvement after review. **Answer:** Demonstrate the indicator, use materials safely and intentionally, and identify one realistic improvement; do not grade personal taste.

- **Official indicator:** ARTS-M2-27 (ศ 3.2) — should_know.
- **Learning outcome:** Students can demonstrate: อธิบายอิทธิพลของวัฒนธรรมที่มีผลต่อเนื้อหาของละคร.
- **Materials:** Board, paper, pencils/colours, safe classroom materials, and relevant audio/visual examples.
- **Teaching sequence:** Introduce a familiar example; model the target skill; guide students through a short task; let students produce or perform independently; use peer feedback and an exit check.

**Practical activity:** Teach through an artwork, listening example or performance appropriate to dance/drama history and cultural meaning. Model safe and respectful handling of materials, instruments, movement space and cultural content. Adapt to lower secondary learners using subject vocabulary and evidence-based analysis.

**Expected evidence:** Three completed tasks demonstrate ARTS-M2-27: accurate observation/listening, explanation or interpretation supported by evidence, and practical creation/performance or contextual response. Accept visual, oral, written, notation-based or performance evidence where it measures the same indicator.

**Teacher check:** Assess (1) accurate subject vocabulary, (2) evidence from the work, score or performance, and (3) intentional practical application or reasoned interpretation. Assess skill and process—not personal taste. Accept culturally respectful equivalent examples.

**Independent practice**
1. **Watch / identify:** View a performance or teacher demonstration related to “อธิบายอิทธิพลของวัฒนธรรมที่มีผลต่อเนื้อหาของละคร”. Identify three features such as movement, gesture, space, rhythm, role, voice, costume, story or cultural form. **Answer:** Identify accurate performance features required by ARTS-M2-27 and support them with something seen or heard; use respectful terminology.
2. **Context / compare:** Compare two dance or drama examples related to “อธิบายอิทธิพลของวัฒนธรรมที่มีผลต่อเนื้อหาของละคร”. Explain a feature shaped by history, community or cultural context. **Answer:** Compare accurately and explain context without stereotypes or ranking traditions.
3. **Present / interpret:** Create a short programme note, movement demonstration or scene plan explaining “อธิบายอิทธิพลของวัฒนธรรมที่มีผลต่อเนื้อหาของละคร” and its cultural context. **Answer:** Explain performance form/context accurately and support interpretation with observable evidence.
