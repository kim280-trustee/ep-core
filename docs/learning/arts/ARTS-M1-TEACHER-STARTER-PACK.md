# Arts M1 — Teacher Starter Pack

**Official OBEC 2021 indicators:** 27 · **Lessons:** 27 · **Assessment prompts:** 81.

## Lesson 1: บรรยายความแตกต่างและความคล้ายคลึงกันของงานทัศนศิลป์และสิ่งแวดล้อมโดยใช้ความรู้เรื่องทัศนธาตุ

**Objective:** `ARTS-M1-01` — บรรยายความแตกต่างและความคล้ายคลึงกันของงานทัศนศิลป์และสิ่งแวดล้อมโดยใช้ความรู้เรื่องทัศนธาตุ

**Arts discipline:** Visual arts
**Grade band:** Lower secondary (M1–M3)
**Indicator focus:** line, colour, shape, form, texture, space and the design choice named in the indicator

- Indicator: ARTS-M1-01 (should_know)
- 50-minute structure: 5-minute stimulus; 10-minute explicit modelling; 20-minute studio, rehearsal or analysis task; 10-minute critique/share; 5-minute exit ticket.
- Evidence: annotated artwork/design, comparative analysis, notation or performance, rehearsal contribution, or evidence-based critique as appropriate.

**Practical activity:** Use two contrasting artworks or visual examples that make line, colour, shape, form, texture, space and the design choice named in the indicator visible. Model how to inspect an example, name the feature and point to evidence. Learners complete a focused studio or analysis task directly addressing “บรรยายความแตกต่างและความคล้ายคลึงกันของงานทัศนศิลป์และสิ่งแวดล้อมโดยใช้ความรู้เรื่องทัศนธาตุ”. Use paper, pencils/colouring tools and safe recycled or modelling materials as needed; demonstrate scissors, adhesives and cutting tools, provide non-toxic materials and keep workspaces clear. For lower secondary learners, annotate evidence, test alternatives and use accurate vocabulary. Explain why a technique or performance choice meets the indicator and identify a specific refinement.

**Expected evidence:** Collect an annotated example and an indicator-matched product: sketch, model, print test, graphic, comparison board or critique sheet. Evidence must show at least two labelled decisions linked to line, colour, shape, form, texture, space and the design choice named in the indicator, one visible feature that meets “บรรยายความแตกต่างและความคล้ายคลึงกันของงานทัศนศิลป์และสิ่งแวดล้อมโดยใช้ความรู้เรื่องทัศนธาตุ”, and a brief explanation or revision note. Accept an oral explanation with teacher checklist when writing is not the target skill.

**Teacher check:** Use a three-point rubric: (1) accurate identification or explanation of line, colour, shape, form, texture, space and the design choice named in the indicator; (2) visible, purposeful use or analysis of the required visual feature; (3) evidence-based explanation or one feasible improvement. Assess against “บรรยายความแตกต่างและความคล้ายคลึงกันของงานทัศนศิลป์และสิ่งแวดล้อมโดยใช้ความรู้เรื่องทัศนธาตุ”, not personal taste, neatness alone or access to expensive materials. Accept safe, low-cost substitutions and culturally respectful interpretations.

**Independent practice**
1. **Knowledge / close observation:** Using the supplied artwork or visual sample, identify two specific features that demonstrate “บรรยายความแตกต่างและความคล้ายคลึงกันของงานทัศนศิลป์และสิ่งแวดล้อมโดยใช้ความรู้เรื่องทัศนธาตุ”. For each, name the relevant part of line, colour, shape, form, texture, space and the design choice named in the indicator and point to the exact visual, audible or performed evidence. **Answer:** Identify two accurate, distinct features; use subject vocabulary; cite evidence for each; connect both features to the indicator rather than offering unsupported opinion.
2. **Application / fictional case:** In a fictional school exhibition design team, learners must demonstrate “บรรยายความแตกต่างและความคล้ายคลึงกันของงานทัศนศิลป์และสิ่งแวดล้อมโดยใช้ความรู้เรื่องทัศนธาตุ”. Explain a three-step plan: identify the challenge, apply line, colour, shape, form, texture, space and the design choice named in the indicator to choose a response, and check whether the result meets the indicator. **Answer:** Give three workable steps in order, make an explicit discipline-specific decision, and justify how observable evidence will show that the indicator has been met.
3. **Demonstration / evidence product:** Produce an annotated study or small visual composition that demonstrates “บรรยายความแตกต่างและความคล้ายคลึงกันของงานทัศนศิลป์และสิ่งแวดล้อมโดยใช้ความรู้เรื่องทัศนธาตุ”. Label or identify two deliberate choices connected to line, colour, shape, form, texture, space and the design choice named in the indicator, then explain how the evidence meets the indicator and what you would refine next. **Answer:** Submit the named product or performance evidence; show two indicator-linked choices; meet the observable criteria in the teacher check; explain one specific strength and one realistic next step.

**Marking (3 points):** 1 point for accurate indicator knowledge or interpretation; 1 point for applying the named discipline skill in the fictional case; 1 point for observable product/performance evidence and a justified reflection. Use the grade-band guidance above; accept accessible response modes that assess the same indicator.
