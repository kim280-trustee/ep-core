# Arts P4 — Teacher Starter Pack

**Curriculum:** Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), OBEC must-know/should-know guide.
**Grade:** P4 · **Official indicators:** 29 · **Lessons:** 29 · **Assessment prompts:** 87

Each lesson uses a 50-minute structure: 5 min warm-up, 10 min teacher modelling, 20 min guided practice or creation, 10 min sharing/performance, 5 min exit assessment. Accept equivalent practical evidence where appropriate.

### Lesson 1: เปรียบเทียบรูปลักษณะของรูปร่าง รูปทรง ในธรรมชาติ สิ่งแวดล้อม และงานทัศนศิลป์

**Objective:** `ARTS-P4-01` — เปรียบเทียบรูปลักษณะของรูปร่าง รูปทรง ในธรรมชาติ สิ่งแวดล้อม และงานทัศนศิลป์

**Arts discipline:** Visual arts
**Grade band:** Upper primary (P4–P6)
**Indicator focus:** volume, form, stable structure, joining methods, surface and spatial arrangement

- **Official indicator:** ARTS-P4-01 (ศ 1.1) — should_know.
- **Learning outcome:** Students demonstrate: เปรียบเทียบรูปลักษณะของรูปร่าง รูปทรง ในธรรมชาติ สิ่งแวดล้อม และงานทัศนศิลป์.
- **Materials:** Board, paper, pencils/colours, safe classroom materials, and relevant audio/visual examples.
- **Teaching sequence:** Introduce a familiar example; model the target skill; guide a short task; let students produce or perform independently; use peer feedback and an exit check.

**Practical activity:** Use two contrasting artworks or visual examples that make volume, form, stable structure, joining methods, surface and spatial arrangement visible. Model how to inspect an example, name the feature and point to evidence. Learners complete a focused studio or analysis task directly addressing “เปรียบเทียบรูปลักษณะของรูปร่าง รูปทรง ในธรรมชาติ สิ่งแวดล้อม และงานทัศนศิลป์”. Use paper, pencils/colouring tools and safe recycled or modelling materials as needed; demonstrate scissors, adhesives and cutting tools, provide non-toxic materials and keep workspaces clear. For upper primary learners, compare two or three examples, introduce subject vocabulary, plan in pairs and explain one practical choice. Provide a model and checklist before independent work.

**Expected evidence:** Collect an annotated example and an indicator-matched product: sketch, model, print test, graphic, comparison board or critique sheet. Evidence must show at least two labelled decisions linked to volume, form, stable structure, joining methods, surface and spatial arrangement, one visible feature that meets “เปรียบเทียบรูปลักษณะของรูปร่าง รูปทรง ในธรรมชาติ สิ่งแวดล้อม และงานทัศนศิลป์”, and a brief explanation or revision note. Accept an oral explanation with teacher checklist when writing is not the target skill.

**Teacher check:** Use a three-point rubric: (1) accurate identification or explanation of volume, form, stable structure, joining methods, surface and spatial arrangement; (2) visible, purposeful use or analysis of the required visual feature; (3) evidence-based explanation or one feasible improvement. Assess against “เปรียบเทียบรูปลักษณะของรูปร่าง รูปทรง ในธรรมชาติ สิ่งแวดล้อม และงานทัศนศิลป์”, not personal taste, neatness alone or access to expensive materials. Accept safe, low-cost substitutions and culturally respectful interpretations.

**Independent practice**
1. **Knowledge / close observation:** Using the supplied artwork or visual sample, identify two specific features that demonstrate “เปรียบเทียบรูปลักษณะของรูปร่าง รูปทรง ในธรรมชาติ สิ่งแวดล้อม และงานทัศนศิลป์”. For each, name the relevant part of volume, form, stable structure, joining methods, surface and spatial arrangement and point to the exact visual, audible or performed evidence. **Answer:** Identify two accurate, distinct features; use subject vocabulary; cite evidence for each; connect both features to the indicator rather than offering unsupported opinion.
2. **Application / fictional case:** In a fictional school exhibition design team, learners must demonstrate “เปรียบเทียบรูปลักษณะของรูปร่าง รูปทรง ในธรรมชาติ สิ่งแวดล้อม และงานทัศนศิลป์”. Explain a three-step plan: identify the challenge, apply volume, form, stable structure, joining methods, surface and spatial arrangement to choose a response, and check whether the result meets the indicator. **Answer:** Give three workable steps in order, make an explicit discipline-specific decision, and justify how observable evidence will show that the indicator has been met.
3. **Demonstration / evidence product:** Produce an annotated study or small visual composition that demonstrates “เปรียบเทียบรูปลักษณะของรูปร่าง รูปทรง ในธรรมชาติ สิ่งแวดล้อม และงานทัศนศิลป์”. Label or identify two deliberate choices connected to volume, form, stable structure, joining methods, surface and spatial arrangement, then explain how the evidence meets the indicator and what you would refine next. **Answer:** Submit the named product or performance evidence; show two indicator-linked choices; meet the observable criteria in the teacher check; explain one specific strength and one realistic next step.

**Marking (3 points):** 1 point for accurate indicator knowledge or interpretation; 1 point for applying the named discipline skill in the fictional case; 1 point for observable product/performance evidence and a justified reflection. Use the grade-band guidance above; accept accessible response modes that assess the same indicator.
