# Arts P4 — Teacher Starter Pack

**Curriculum:** Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), OBEC must-know/should-know guide.
**Grade:** P4 · **Official indicators:** 29 · **Lessons:** 29 · **Assessment prompts:** 87

Each lesson uses a 50-minute structure: 5 min warm-up, 10 min teacher modelling, 20 min guided practice or creation, 10 min sharing/performance, 5 min exit assessment. Accept equivalent practical evidence where appropriate.
