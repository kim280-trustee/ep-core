# Arts P5 — Teacher Starter Pack

**Curriculum:** Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), OBEC must-know/should-know indicator guide.
**Grade:** P5 · **Official indicators:** 26 · **Lessons:** 26 · **Assessment prompts:** 78

Each lesson uses a 50-minute structure: 5 min warm-up/stimulus, 10 min explicit modelling, 20 min guided practice or creation, 10 min sharing/performance, 5 min exit assessment. Teachers should accept equivalent practical evidence where appropriate.

### Lesson 1: บรรยายเกี่ยวกับจังหวะ ตำแหน่งของสิ่งต่าง ๆ ที่ปรากฏในสิ่งแวดล้อมและงานทัศนศิลป์

**Objective:** `ARTS-P5-01` — บรรยายเกี่ยวกับจังหวะ ตำแหน่งของสิ่งต่าง ๆ ที่ปรากฏในสิ่งแวดล้อมและงานทัศนศิลป์

**Arts discipline:** Visual arts
**Grade band:** Upper primary (P4–P6)
**Indicator focus:** unity, harmony, balance, proportion, repetition and visual rhythm

- **Official indicator:** ARTS-P5-01 (ศ 1.1) — should_know.
- **Learning outcome:** Students can demonstrate: บรรยายเกี่ยวกับจังหวะ ตำแหน่งของสิ่งต่าง ๆ ที่ปรากฏในสิ่งแวดล้อมและงานทัศนศิลป์.
- **Materials:** Board, paper, pencils/colours, safe classroom materials, and relevant audio/visual examples.
- **Teaching sequence:** Introduce a familiar example; model the target skill; guide students through a short task; let students produce or perform independently; use peer feedback and an exit check.

**Practical activity:** Use two contrasting artworks or visual examples that make unity, harmony, balance, proportion, repetition and visual rhythm visible. Model how to inspect an example, name the feature and point to evidence. Learners complete a focused studio or analysis task directly addressing “บรรยายเกี่ยวกับจังหวะ ตำแหน่งของสิ่งต่าง ๆ ที่ปรากฏในสิ่งแวดล้อมและงานทัศนศิลป์”. Use paper, pencils/colouring tools and safe recycled or modelling materials as needed; demonstrate scissors, adhesives and cutting tools, provide non-toxic materials and keep workspaces clear. For upper primary learners, compare two or three examples, introduce subject vocabulary, plan in pairs and explain one practical choice. Provide a model and checklist before independent work.

**Expected evidence:** Collect an annotated example and an indicator-matched product: sketch, model, print test, graphic, comparison board or critique sheet. Evidence must show at least two labelled decisions linked to unity, harmony, balance, proportion, repetition and visual rhythm, one visible feature that meets “บรรยายเกี่ยวกับจังหวะ ตำแหน่งของสิ่งต่าง ๆ ที่ปรากฏในสิ่งแวดล้อมและงานทัศนศิลป์”, and a brief explanation or revision note. Accept an oral explanation with teacher checklist when writing is not the target skill.

**Teacher check:** Use a three-point rubric: (1) accurate identification or explanation of unity, harmony, balance, proportion, repetition and visual rhythm; (2) visible, purposeful use or analysis of the required visual feature; (3) evidence-based explanation or one feasible improvement. Assess against “บรรยายเกี่ยวกับจังหวะ ตำแหน่งของสิ่งต่าง ๆ ที่ปรากฏในสิ่งแวดล้อมและงานทัศนศิลป์”, not personal taste, neatness alone or access to expensive materials. Accept safe, low-cost substitutions and culturally respectful interpretations.

**Independent practice**
1. **Knowledge / close observation:** Using the supplied artwork or visual sample, identify two specific features that demonstrate “บรรยายเกี่ยวกับจังหวะ ตำแหน่งของสิ่งต่าง ๆ ที่ปรากฏในสิ่งแวดล้อมและงานทัศนศิลป์”. For each, name the relevant part of unity, harmony, balance, proportion, repetition and visual rhythm and point to the exact visual, audible or performed evidence. **Answer:** Identify two accurate, distinct features; use subject vocabulary; cite evidence for each; connect both features to the indicator rather than offering unsupported opinion.
2. **Application / fictional case:** In a fictional school exhibition design team, learners must demonstrate “บรรยายเกี่ยวกับจังหวะ ตำแหน่งของสิ่งต่าง ๆ ที่ปรากฏในสิ่งแวดล้อมและงานทัศนศิลป์”. Explain a three-step plan: identify the challenge, apply unity, harmony, balance, proportion, repetition and visual rhythm to choose a response, and check whether the result meets the indicator. **Answer:** Give three workable steps in order, make an explicit discipline-specific decision, and justify how observable evidence will show that the indicator has been met.
3. **Demonstration / evidence product:** Produce an annotated study or small visual composition that demonstrates “บรรยายเกี่ยวกับจังหวะ ตำแหน่งของสิ่งต่าง ๆ ที่ปรากฏในสิ่งแวดล้อมและงานทัศนศิลป์”. Label or identify two deliberate choices connected to unity, harmony, balance, proportion, repetition and visual rhythm, then explain how the evidence meets the indicator and what you would refine next. **Answer:** Submit the named product or performance evidence; show two indicator-linked choices; meet the observable criteria in the teacher check; explain one specific strength and one realistic next step.

**Marking (3 points):** 1 point for accurate indicator knowledge or interpretation; 1 point for applying the named discipline skill in the fictional case; 1 point for observable product/performance evidence and a justified reflection. Use the grade-band guidance above; accept accessible response modes that assess the same indicator.
