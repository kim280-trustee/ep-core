## Official standards

The subject uses the eight Foreign Languages standards in the OBEC framework:

- ต 1.1 — Listening and reading comprehension
- ต 1.2 — Interpersonal communication
- ต 1.3 — Presenting information and opinions
- ต 2.1 — Language, culture and social conventions
- ต 2.2 — Comparing language and culture
- ต 3.1 — Cross-curricular language learning
- ต 4.1 — Using English in real-life situations
- ต 4.2 — Research, further study and career use

The unused non-standard `ต 1.4` topic from the initial scaffold was removed. All 193 objectives now sit under the eight official standards.
