# Foreign Languages Teacher Starter Pack — M1

**Curriculum:** Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), Foreign Languages (English)
**Coverage:** 20 official indicators; 20 lesson starters; 60 original answer-keyed practice questions
**Official source:** OBEC 2021 Must-Know / Should-Know compilation, Foreign Languages (English), printed page 352. Each lesson preserves the exact Thai indicator and official indicator code from the live curriculum catalogue and source reconciliation register.

> **Priority note:** Must-Know / Should-Know labels are historical OBEC 2021 COVID-period classifications, not a statement of permanent current policy. Teach all curriculum indicators appropriately and follow the school's approved curriculum.

## Teacher guidance

- Use the original Thai indicator wording and exact objective code to preserve catalogue alignment.
- Middle-school tasks progress from supported practice in M1 to more connected, independent communication and evidence-based responses in M2–M3.
- Use age-appropriate factual source materials for news, community and cultural topics; distinguish fact from opinion and identify sources.
- For cultural comparisons, use accurate descriptive evidence. Never require worship, religious participation or disclosure of personal beliefs. Offer a neutral research or language-production alternative.
- Do not assume that all learners celebrate the same events or share the same family or community experiences.
- Answer keys are sample evidence, not exclusive wording. Accept other accurate responses that meet the indicator.

## Indicator lessons

### 1. Instructions and requests

- **Official indicator code:** ต 1.1 ม.1/1
- **Standard:** ต 1.1
- **Priority classification:** must_know
- **Official indicator:** ปฏิบัติตามคำสั่ง คำขอร้อง คำแนะนำ และคำชี้แจงง่าย ๆ ที่ฟังและอ่าน
**Objective:** `FL-M1-01` — ปฏิบัติตามคำสั่ง คำขอร้อง คำแนะนำ และคำชี้แจงง่าย ๆ ที่ฟังและอ่าน
**Learning outcomes:** Learners demonstrate instructions and requests in an age-appropriate context through an observable spoken or written response.
**Practical activity:** Use familiar school, home and community situations. Model language and provide short texts, visual prompts and sentence frames before independent practice. Use a short teacher-created text, notice, dialogue or visual. Model the listening or reading strategy, then ask learners to identify evidence and complete an individual response.
- **Resources:** Teacher-approved short texts, dialogues, notices, information cards, relevant visuals and learner response sheets.

**Independent practice**
1. Follow this instruction: “Open your book, read page 8 and underline two key words.” List the three actions in order. **Answer:** Open the book; read page 8; underline two key words.
2. A teacher says, “Please work with a partner.” What should you do? **Answer:** Work with one partner.
3. Write one polite classroom instruction for a classmate. **Answer:** A clear instruction such as “Please close the door.”
