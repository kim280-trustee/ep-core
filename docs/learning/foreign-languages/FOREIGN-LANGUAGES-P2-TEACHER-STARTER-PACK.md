# Foreign Languages Teacher Starter Pack — Primary 2

**Curriculum:** Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), Foreign Languages (English)
**Coverage:** 16 official indicators; 16 lesson starters; 48 original answer-keyed practice questions
**Source alignment:** Each lesson retains its live catalogue objective code, standard and original Thai indicator wording. See the Foreign Languages indicator source reconciliation register for the OBEC source page and 2021 priority label.

> **Priority note:** Must-Know / Should-Know labels are historical OBEC 2021 COVID-period classifications, not a statement of permanent current policy. Teach all curriculum indicators appropriately and follow the school's approved curriculum.

## Teacher guidance

- Build on Primary 1 language, adding short phrases and simple sentences. Model pronunciation, stress and word order before independent work.
- Use short instructions, simple stories, pictures and real classroom objects. Check comprehension before asking learners to read or write.
- Accept pointing, matching, drawing, acting and short oral or written responses when they measure the indicator.
- Teach cultural examples respectfully and descriptively. Never require worship, religious participation or disclosure of a learner's beliefs. Offer an equivalent non-festival activity.
- Use the answer keys as acceptable evidence, not as the only possible wording.

## Indicator lessons

### 1. Follow simple spoken commands and requests

- **Standard:** ต 1.1
- **Official indicator code:** ต 1.1 ป.2/1
- **Priority classification:** must_know
- **Official indicator:** ปฏิบัติตามคำสั่งและคำขอร้องง่าย ๆ ที่ฟัง
**Objective:** `FL-P2-01` — ปฏิบัติตามคำสั่งและคำขอร้องง่าย ๆ ที่ฟัง
**Learning outcomes:** Learners follow familiar classroom commands and polite one-step requests.
**Practical activity:** Model “Open your book”, “Show me a pencil” and “Please come here”. Learners listen, select the matching picture and carry out the action.
- **Resources:** Classroom objects and command/request cards.

**Independent practice**
1. Listen: “Show me a ruler.” What should you hold up? **Answer:** A ruler.
2. Listen: “Please close your book.” What action should you do? **Answer:** Close the book.
3. Give the correct action for “Don’t talk in class.” **Answer:** Stop talking and remain quiet.

### 2. Read and spell simple words and sentences

- **Standard:** ต 1.1
- **Official indicator code:** ต 1.1 ป.2/1
- **Priority classification:** must_know
- **Official indicator:** ระบุตัวอักษรและเสียง อ่านออกเสียงคำ สะกดคำ และอ่านประโยคง่าย ๆ ถูกต้องตามหลักการอ่าน
**Objective:** `FL-P2-02` — ระบุตัวอักษรและเสียง อ่านออกเสียงคำ สะกดคำ และอ่านประโยคง่าย ๆ ถูกต้องตามหลักการอ่าน
**Learning outcomes:** Learners use letter names and sounds to read and spell familiar words and short sentences.
**Practical activity:** Practise initial and final consonant sounds and simple word stress. Learners build words with letter tiles, read them aloud and place them in a short model sentence.
- **Resources:** Letter tiles, word cards and short sentence strips.

**Independent practice**
1. Which word ends with /t/: “cat” or “bag”? **Answer:** “cat”.
2. Put the letters in order to spell “fish”: H, F, S, I. **Answer:** F-I-S-H.
3. Read: “This is a red pen.” Identify the colour word. **Answer:** “red”.

### 3. Match heard words, phrases and sentences to pictures

- **Standard:** ต 1.1
- **Official indicator code:** ต 1.1 ป.2/1
- **Priority classification:** must_know
- **Official indicator:** เลือกภาพตรงตามความหมายของคำ กลุ่มคำ และประโยคที่ฟัง
**Objective:** `FL-P2-03` — เลือกภาพตรงตามความหมายของคำ กลุ่มคำ และประโยคที่ฟัง
**Learning outcomes:** Learners select a picture matching a spoken word, phrase or simple sentence.
**Practical activity:** Use familiar vocabulary from home, school, food, drink and recreation. Say a phrase or sentence such as “three green apples”; learners select the exact picture.
- **Resources:** Picture sets with clear differences in number, colour and object.

**Independent practice**
1. The teacher says “two yellow bananas”. Choose the matching picture. **Answer:** The picture with two yellow bananas.
2. The teacher says “a girl under the tree”. Which picture is correct? **Answer:** The picture showing a girl under a tree.
3. The teacher says “The boys are playing football.” Choose the matching action picture. **Answer:** The picture showing boys playing football.

### 4. Answer questions about a short listening text

- **Standard:** ต 1.1
- **Official indicator code:** ต 1.1 ป.2/1
- **Priority classification:** must_know
- **Official indicator:** ตอบคำถามจากการฟังประโยค บทสนทนา หรือนิทานง่าย ๆ ที่มีภาพประกอบ
**Objective:** `FL-P2-04` — ตอบคำถามจากการฟังประโยค บทสนทนา หรือนิทานง่าย ๆ ที่มีภาพประกอบ
**Learning outcomes:** Learners answer who, what, where or yes/no questions about a short illustrated text.
**Practical activity:** Read a short story twice. Learners first order three pictures, then answer a question using a word or simple sentence.
- **Resources:** Three-picture story sequence and question cards.

**Independent practice**
1. Listen: “Nina goes to the market with her mother.” Who goes with Nina? **Answer:** Her mother.
2. Listen: “They buy oranges.” What do they buy? **Answer:** Oranges.
3. Listen: “The oranges are in a bag.” Where are the oranges? **Answer:** In a bag.

### 5. Exchange short expressions in everyday conversations

- **Standard:** ต 1.2
- **Official indicator code:** ต 1.2 ป.2/1
- **Priority classification:** must_know
- **Official indicator:** พูดโต้ตอบด้วยคำสั้น ๆ ง่าย ๆ ในการสื่อสารระหว่างบุคคลตามแบบที่ฟัง
**Objective:** `FL-P2-05` — พูดโต้ตอบด้วยคำสั้น ๆ ง่าย ๆ ในการสื่อสารระหว่างบุคคลตามแบบที่ฟัง
**Learning outcomes:** Learners take part in a short modelled exchange using greetings, thanks, apologies and leave-taking.
**Practical activity:** Model a two-turn conversation. Learners practise changing the greeting and response while keeping the exchange natural.
- **Resources:** Dialogue strips and partner cards.

**Independent practice**
1. A classmate says “Good morning.” Give a suitable response. **Answer:** “Good morning.”
2. You bump into someone by mistake. What can you say? **Answer:** “I’m sorry.”
3. Someone says “Thank you.” Give a suitable response. **Answer:** “You’re welcome.”

### 7. Express simple needs using a model

- **Standard:** ต 1.2
- **Official indicator code:** ต 1.2 ป.2/1
- **Priority classification:** should_know
- **Official indicator:** บอกความต้องการง่าย ๆ ของตนเองตามแบบที่ฟัง
**Objective:** `FL-P2-07` — บอกความต้องการง่าย ๆ ของตนเองตามแบบที่ฟัง
**Learning outcomes:** Learners express a simple need using “I want…” or “Can I have…?” with a polite word.
**Practical activity:** Use picture prompts for water, food, a pencil and help. Learners select a need and practise a complete request.
- **Resources:** Need cards and classroom objects.

**Independent practice**
1. You need water. Make a polite request. **Answer:** “Can I have some water, please?”
2. You want a blue pencil. Say what you want. **Answer:** “I want a blue pencil, please.”
3. You need help with a task. What can you say? **Answer:** “Can you help me, please?”

### 8. Ask for and give simple personal information

- **Standard:** ต 1.2
- **Official indicator code:** ต 1.2 ป.2/1
- **Priority classification:** must_know
- **Official indicator:** พูดขอและให้ข้อมูลง่าย ๆ เกี่ยวกับตนเองตามแบบที่ฟัง
**Objective:** `FL-P2-08` — พูดขอและให้ข้อมูลง่าย ๆ เกี่ยวกับตนเองตามแบบที่ฟัง
**Learning outcomes:** Learners ask and answer modelled questions about a name and other simple personal details.
**Practical activity:** Use fictional profile cards. Model “What’s your name?”, “How old are you?” and “Where are you from?” Learners ask and answer in pairs.
- **Resources:** Fictional profile cards and question strips.

**Independent practice**
1. Ask a partner for their name. **Answer:** “What’s your name?”
2. Profile card: Name = Mali. Answer “What’s your name?” **Answer:** “My name is Mali.”
3. Profile card: Age = eight. Answer “How old are you?” **Answer:** “I am eight years old.”

### 9. Give information about yourself and familiar topics

- **Standard:** ต 1.3
- **Official indicator code:** ต 1.3 ป.2/1
- **Priority classification:** must_know
- **Official indicator:** พูดให้ข้อมูลเกี่ยวกับตนเองและเรื่องใกล้ตัว
**Objective:** `FL-P2-09` — พูดให้ข้อมูลเกี่ยวกับตนเองและเรื่องใกล้ตัว
**Learning outcomes:** Learners give short information about themselves, familiar people and objects using numbers, colours, size and position words.
**Practical activity:** Learners describe a picture using two or three simple sentences. Include age, colour, number or position.
- **Resources:** Familiar scene pictures and vocabulary prompts.

**Independent practice**
1. Say a sentence about a person in a picture: name or age. **Answer:** Any accurate modelled sentence, such as “He is eight.”
2. Describe two objects using colour and number. **Answer:** A correct sentence such as “There are two red bags.”
3. Describe where an object is using “in”, “on” or “under”. **Answer:** A correct sentence matching the picture, such as “The ball is under the table.”

### 10. Use gestures that fit a familiar social situation

- **Standard:** ต 2.1
- **Official indicator code:** ต 2.1 ป.2/1
- **Priority classification:** should_know
- **Official indicator:** พูดและทำท่าประกอบตามวัฒนธรรมของเจ้าของภาษา
**Objective:** `FL-P2-10` — พูดและทำท่าประกอบตามวัฒนธรรมของเจ้าของภาษา
**Learning outcomes:** Learners choose a simple gesture that supports a greeting, thanks or leave-taking.
**Practical activity:** Compare picture examples of waving, smiling, nodding and other gestures. Discuss that gestures vary across people and contexts. Practise non-contact greetings.
- **Resources:** Gesture picture cards and short model dialogues.

**Independent practice**
1. Choose a suitable gesture for “Goodbye”. **Answer:** A wave.
2. Show a respectful way to listen when a classmate speaks. **Answer:** Face the speaker, listen quietly and avoid interrupting.
3. Give a greeting using words and a suitable gesture. **Answer:** A greeting such as “Hello” with a friendly wave or smile.

### 11. Name vocabulary linked to important festivals

- **Standard:** ต 2.1
- **Official indicator code:** ต 2.1 ป.2/1
- **Priority classification:** should_know
- **Official indicator:** บอกชื่อและคำศัพท์เกี่ยวกับเทศกาลสำคัญของเจ้าของภาษา
**Objective:** `FL-P2-11` — บอกชื่อและคำศัพท์เกี่ยวกับเทศกาลสำคัญของเจ้าของภาษา
**Learning outcomes:** Learners identify familiar vocabulary connected to festivals and everyday life in English-speaking contexts.
**Practical activity:** Use neutral information cards about Christmas and New Year, including food, clothing, greetings and seasonal words. Invite comparisons without suggesting everyone celebrates.
- **Resources:** Festival vocabulary cards and simple information pictures.

**Independent practice**
1. Name one English word associated with Christmas. **Answer:** Any taught word, such as “tree”, “star”, “card” or “gift”.
2. Which greeting matches New Year? **Answer:** “Happy New Year.”
3. Name one item people may use at a celebration. **Answer:** A suitable word such as “card”, “food”, “music” or “decoration”.

**Learning outcomes:** Learners take part in a suitable language or cultural activity and use at least one relevant phrase.

3. Complete a chosen language activity and share one thing you learned. **Answer:** A completed activity and a relevant word or short sentence.

### 13. Identify letters and sounds in English and Thai

- **Standard:** ต 2.2
- **Official indicator code:** ต 2.2 ป.2/1
- **Priority classification:** should_know
- **Official indicator:** ระบุตัวอักษรและเสียงตัวอักษรของภาษาต่างประเทศและภาษาไทย
**Objective:** `FL-P2-13` — ระบุตัวอักษรและเสียงตัวอักษรของภาษาต่างประเทศและภาษาไทย
**Learning outcomes:** Learners identify taught English letters and sounds and recognise that Thai and English letter-sound systems are not identical.
**Practical activity:** Sort English and Thai character cards. Say each sound in its own language and identify one difference without forcing one-to-one equivalence.
- **Resources:** Letter cards, Thai character cards and sound prompts.

**Independent practice**
1. Find the English letter that begins “fish”. **Answer:** F.
2. Say the initial sound in “map”. **Answer:** /m/.
3. Name one difference between the English and Thai writing systems shown on the cards. **Answer:** Any accurate observation, such as different letter shapes or different sound-letter relationships.

### 16. Collect familiar vocabulary from simple media

- **Standard:** ต 4.2
- **Official indicator code:** ต 4.2 ป.2/1
- **Priority classification:** should_know
- **Official indicator:** ใช้ภาษาต่างประเทศเพื่อรวบรวมคำศัพท์ที่เกี่ยวข้องใกล้ตัว
**Objective:** `FL-P2-16` — ใช้ภาษาต่างประเทศเพื่อรวบรวมคำศัพท์ที่เกี่ยวข้องใกล้ตัว
**Learning outcomes:** Learners collect, record and share a small set of familiar English words from approved media.
**Practical activity:** Use teacher-selected picture books, packaging labels or classroom displays. Learners record five words, draw or match each meaning and share two words with a partner.
- **Resources:** Approved printed media and a simple vocabulary collection sheet.

**Independent practice**
1. Copy one English word from a teacher-selected label. **Answer:** An accurately copied word from the approved label.
2. Match the word “milk” to its picture. **Answer:** The milk picture.
3. Record five familiar words from an approved picture book or display. **Answer:** Five accurately copied words with matching pictures or meanings.
