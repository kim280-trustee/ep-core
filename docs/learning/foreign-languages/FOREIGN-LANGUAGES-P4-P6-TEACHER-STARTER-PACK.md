# Foreign Languages P4–P6 — Teacher Starter Pack

**Curriculum:** Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), revised B.E. 2560 (2017)  
**Subject:** Foreign Languages — English (`FOREIGN-LANGUAGES`)  
**Unique indicators:** 60  
**OBEC 2021 priority:** 55 must-know; 5 should-know  
**Assessment material:** 180 formative tasks with expected-answer guidance

Each lesson preserves the official Thai indicator wording and code. The activities and formative tasks are designed for English-language instruction; teachers should adapt vocabulary, text length and speaking demands to the class.

## Teaching guidance

- Use comprehensible instructions, modelling, visual support and frequent opportunities for learners to speak and listen.
- Build reading and writing from age-appropriate vocabulary and sentence frames toward longer authentic texts and presentations.
- Teach cultural content respectfully, avoid stereotypes and distinguish a cultural example from a universal rule.
- For online research, use teacher-approved sources and teach learners to record where information came from.

## Indicator lessons

### 1. FL-P4-01 — ปฏิบัติตามคำสั่ง คำขอร้อง และคำแนะนำ (instructions) ง่าย ๆ ที่ฟังหรืออ่าน

**Objective:** `FL-P4-01` — ปฏิบัติตามคำสั่ง คำขอร้อง และคำแนะนำ (instructions) ง่าย ๆ ที่ฟังหรืออ่าน

- **Official indicator:** `ต 1.1 ป.4/1`
- **Standard:** ต 1.1 — Listening and reading comprehension
- **OBEC 2021 priority:** `must_know`
- **Original Thai indicator:** ปฏิบัติตามคำสั่ง คำขอร้อง และคำแนะนำ (instructions) ง่าย ๆ ที่ฟังหรืออ่าน

**Teaching activity:** Use short dialogues, texts, chants and story cards. Practise reading aloud, following instructions, identifying details and explaining the main idea.

**Practical activity:** Practise listening, reading, pronunciation, vocabulary and comprehension through a teacher-approved audio, text, dialogue or real-life scenario. Model the target language and give learners structured speaking/listening/reading/writing practice. Adapt to upper primary learners using short texts, complete sentences and simple exchanges.

**Expected evidence:** Three tasks demonstrate FL-P4-01: accurate understanding, appropriate language use or interpretation, and a spoken/written performance or product. Accept equivalent correct language and accessible response formats while retaining the indicator's skill target.

**Teacher check:** Assess (1) meaning and task accuracy, (2) vocabulary/grammar/pronunciation or language choices appropriate to the grade, and (3) communication with the intended audience. For open-ended speaking/writing, accept natural equivalent answers; assess clarity and relevance rather than one memorised sentence.

**Independent practice**
1. **Listen / read:** Use an age-appropriate audio clip or text related to “ปฏิบัติตามคำสั่ง คำขอร้อง และคำแนะนำ (instructions) ง่าย ๆ ที่ฟังหรืออ่าน”. Identify the exact instruction, sounds, words, main idea, sequence or details required by the indicator. **Answer:** The learner correctly identifies or follows the language information required by FL-P4-01; accept an equivalent clear response at the expected grade level.
2. **Understand / interpret:** Answer a question about the same source connected to “ปฏิบัติตามคำสั่ง คำขอร้อง และคำแนะนำ (instructions) ง่าย ๆ ที่ฟังหรืออ่าน”. Explain the meaning or inference and point to the word, phrase or detail that supports the answer. **Answer:** The answer demonstrates comprehension and is supported by relevant evidence from the audio/text; do not accept unsupported guesses.
3. **Demonstrate:** Read aloud, follow a set of instructions, retell or complete a short comprehension task that demonstrates “ปฏิบัติตามคำสั่ง คำขอร้อง และคำแนะนำ (instructions) ง่าย ๆ ที่ฟังหรืออ่าน”. **Answer:** The learner completes the task accurately, communicates the intended meaning and uses pronunciation/reading strategies appropriate to the grade.
