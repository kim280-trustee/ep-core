# Foreign Languages P4–P6 — Teacher Starter Pack

**Curriculum:** Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), revised B.E. 2560 (2017)  
**Subject:** Foreign Languages — English (`FOREIGN-LANGUAGES`)  
**Unique indicators:** 60  
**OBEC 2021 priority:** 55 must-know; 5 should-know  
**Assessment material:** 180 formative tasks with expected-answer guidance

Each lesson preserves the official Thai indicator wording and code. The activities and formative tasks are designed for English-language instruction; teachers should adapt vocabulary, text length and speaking demands to the class.

## Teaching guidance

- Use comprehensible instructions, modelling, visual support and frequent opportunities for learners to speak and listen.
- Build reading and writing from age-appropriate vocabulary and sentence frames toward longer authentic texts and presentations.
- Teach cultural content respectfully, avoid stereotypes and distinguish a cultural example from a universal rule.
- For online research, use teacher-approved sources and teach learners to record where information came from.
