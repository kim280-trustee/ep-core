# Foreign Languages M1–M3 — Teacher Starter Pack

**Curriculum:** Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), revised B.E. 2560 (2017)  
**Subject:** Foreign Languages — English (`FOREIGN-LANGUAGES`)  
**Unique indicators:** 62  
**OBEC 2021 priority:** 57 must-know; 5 should-know  
**Assessment material:** 186 formative tasks with expected-answer guidance

## Teaching guidance

- Use age-appropriate authentic texts and topics; scaffold vocabulary and language structures before independent tasks.
- Assess listening, speaking, reading and writing through a balanced mix of individual and collaborative tasks.
- Teach respectful cultural comparison and avoid stereotypes or unsupported generalisations.
- For research tasks, use approved sources and require learners to record and evaluate where information came from.

### 1. FL-M1-01 — ปฏิบัติตามคำสั่ง คำขอร้อง คำแนะนำ และคำชี้แจงง่าย ๆ ที่ฟังและอ่าน

- **Official indicator:** `ต 1.1 ม.1/1`
- **Standard:** ต 1.1 — Interpretation: listening and reading
- **OBEC 2021 priority:** `must_know`
- **Original Thai indicator:** ปฏิบัติตามคำสั่ง คำขอร้อง คำแนะนำ และคำชี้แจงง่าย ๆ ที่ฟังและอ่าน

**Teaching activity:** Use age-appropriate news, stories, announcements and digital media. Learners practise skimming, scanning, inference and evidence-based comprehension.

**Formative assessment and answer guidance**

1. **Comprehension — Read or listen to a text linked to this indicator. Identify its topic, main idea and two supporting details.**  
   **Expected answer/evidence:** The response identifies the central idea and accurate supporting evidence from the source.
2. **Interpretation — What can be inferred about the speaker, writer, purpose or situation?**  
   **Expected answer/evidence:** The learner makes a reasonable inference and supports it with evidence from the text.
3. **Critical response — Summarise the message and give a reasoned response to it.**  
   **Expected answer/evidence:** The summary is accurate; the response is relevant and supported by a reason or example.
