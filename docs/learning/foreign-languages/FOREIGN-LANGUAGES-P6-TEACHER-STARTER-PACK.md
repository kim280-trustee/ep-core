# Foreign Languages Teacher Starter Pack — Primary 6

**Curriculum:** Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), Foreign Languages (English)
**Coverage:** 20 official indicators; 20 lesson starters; 60 original answer-keyed practice questions
**Official source:** OBEC 2021 Must-Know / Should-Know compilation, Foreign Languages (English), printed page 348. The source register records the exact Thai indicator, standard, grade, priority label and page reference.

> **Priority note:** Must-Know / Should-Know labels are historical OBEC 2021 COVID-period classifications, not a statement of permanent current policy. Teach all curriculum indicators appropriately and follow the school's approved curriculum.

## Teacher guidance

- Use the original Thai indicator wording and exact objective code to preserve catalogue alignment.
- Teach English through familiar Thai school, home and community contexts, with clear modelling and age-appropriate scaffolding.
- Integrate listening, speaking, reading and writing where appropriate; do not assess an unrelated skill instead of the stated indicator.
- For cultural comparisons, use accurate, descriptive evidence. Never require worship, religious participation or disclosure of personal beliefs. Offer an equivalent non-festival route to demonstrate the same language skill.
- Do not assume that all learners celebrate the same events or have the same family structure. Accept different accurate examples.
- Answer keys are sample evidence, not exclusive wording. Accept other accurate responses that meet the indicator.

## Indicator lessons

### 1. Follow multi-step instructions and advice

- **Standard:** ต 1.1
- **Priority classification:** must_know
- **Official indicator:** ปฏิบัติตามคำสั่ง คำขอร้อง และคำแนะนำที่ฟังและอ่าน
**Objective:** `FL-P6-01` — ปฏิบัติตามคำสั่ง คำขอร้อง และคำแนะนำที่ฟังและอ่าน
**Learning outcomes:** Learners follow multi-step instructions and advice through a short, observable English-language task in a familiar, age-appropriate context.
**Practical activity:** Let learners plan a short response independently, use a reference card if needed, and explain one choice. Model a sequence of spoken and written instructions. Learners order instruction cards, carry them out, then write one instruction for a partner.
- **Resources:** Teacher-created situation cards, short model text or dialogue, board and learner response sheets.

**Independent practice**
1. Listen to “Turn to page 12, underline the title, then compare with your partner.” What are the three actions? **Answer:** Turn to page 12, underline the title, and compare with a partner.
2. Read aloud: “Our class visits the library every Friday.” Which word tells when? **Answer:** Every Friday.
3. A sign shows a person washing hands. Which sentence matches: “Wash your hands” or “Close the window”? **Answer:** Wash your hands.

### 2. Read short texts, stories and poems aloud

- **Standard:** ต 1.1
- **Priority classification:** must_know
- **Official indicator:** อ่านออกเสียงข้อความ นิทาน และบทกลอนสั้น ๆ ถูกต้องตามหลักการอ่าน
**Objective:** `FL-P6-02` — อ่านออกเสียงข้อความ นิทาน และบทกลอนสั้น ๆ ถูกต้องตามหลักการอ่าน
**Learning outcomes:** Learners read short texts, stories and poems aloud through a short, observable English-language task in a familiar, age-appropriate context.
**Practical activity:** Let learners plan a short response independently, use a reference card if needed, and explain one choice. Model target sounds, word stress and punctuation. Learners rehearse a short text in pairs, mark pauses and read it aloud.
- **Resources:** Teacher-created situation cards, short model text or dialogue, board and learner response sheets.

**Independent practice**
1. Read aloud: “The bright green leaves moved gently in the wind.” Keep the final sounds clear. **Answer:** The sentence is read clearly with the final sounds audible.
2. Read the short rhyme: “I see a bee beside a tree.” Which words rhyme? **Answer:** Bee and tree.
3. Read “The train leaves at nine.” Which word has the long /a/ sound? **Answer:** Train.

**Learning outcomes:** Learners communicate independently in school contexts through a short, observable English-language task in a familiar, age-appropriate context.
**Practical activity:** Let learners plan a short response independently, use a reference card if needed, and explain one choice. Role-play realistic school situations such as asking directions, borrowing materials or requesting repetition. Learners complete the exchange politely.
- **Resources:** Teacher-created situation cards, short model text or dialogue, board and learner response sheets.

**Independent practice**
1. A visitor asks where the library is. Give a helpful direction. **Answer:** A clear direction, such as “Go straight and turn left.”
2. Ask a teacher politely to repeat an instruction. **Answer:** “Could you repeat that, please?”
3. End a school conversation politely. **Answer:** A suitable closing, such as “Thank you for your help.”

### 20. Use English sources to research and organise information

- **Standard:** ต 4.2
- **Priority classification:** must_know
- **Official indicator:** ใช้ภาษาต่างประเทศในการสืบค้นและรวบรวมข้อมูลต่าง ๆ
**Objective:** `FL-P6-20` — ใช้ภาษาต่างประเทศในการสืบค้นและรวบรวมข้อมูลต่าง ๆ
**Learning outcomes:** Learners use English sources to research and organise information through a short, observable English-language task in a familiar, age-appropriate context.
**Practical activity:** Let learners plan a short response independently, use a reference card if needed, and explain one choice. Use a teacher-approved book or child-safe source. Learners record facts or vocabulary, note the source and present the findings.
- **Resources:** Teacher-created situation cards, short model text or dialogue, board and learner response sheets.

**Independent practice**
1. Find one fact in a teacher-approved English source and record it. **Answer:** One accurate fact copied or paraphrased from the approved source.
2. Record the source title or name. **Answer:** The correct title or name of the source.
3. Present the fact and explain why the source was useful. **Answer:** A short accurate presentation with a relevant explanation.
