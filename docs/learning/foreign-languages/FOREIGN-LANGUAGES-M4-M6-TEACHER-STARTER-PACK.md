# Foreign Languages Teacher Starter Pack — M4–M6 Shared Interval

**Curriculum:** Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), Foreign Languages (English)
**Coverage:** 21 shared upper-secondary indicators; 21 lesson starters; 63 original answer-keyed practice questions
**Grade alignment:** One shared M4–M6 interval pack. Each objective code is the single catalogue record aligned to M4, M5 and M6; do not create three copies.
**Official source:** OBEC 2021 Must-Know / Should-Know compilation, Foreign Languages (English), printed page 367. Each lesson preserves the exact Thai indicator and official indicator code from the live curriculum catalogue and source register.

 > **Priority note:** Must-Know / Should-Know labels are historical OBEC 2021 COVID-period classifications, not permanent current policy. Teach all curriculum indicators appropriately and follow the school's approved curriculum.

## Teacher guidance

- Use the same 21 shared interval indicators for M4, M5 and M6; differentiate text complexity, independence, research depth and audience expectations rather than duplicating the objective records.
- M4 may use scaffolded source comparison and presentation planning; M5 should increase independent synthesis and audience adaptation; M6 should emphasise sustained independent research, evaluation and polished communication.
- Use reliable, dated sources for news, cultural, health, education and career information. Distinguish fact, opinion, inference and uncertainty.
- Cultural inquiry must be descriptive and inclusive. Never require worship, religious participation or disclosure of personal beliefs; provide a neutral language/research alternative.
- Accept other accurate responses that satisfy the indicator. Answer keys are examples of evidence, not exclusive wording.

## Indicator lessons

### 1. Using manuals and complex instructions

- **Official indicator code:** ต 1.1 ม.4-6/1
- **Standard:** ต 1.1
- **Priority classification:** must_know
- **Official indicator:** ปฏิบัติตามคำแนะนำในคู่มือการใช้งานต่าง ๆ คำชี้แจง คำอธิบาย และคำบรรยายที่ฟังและอ่าน
**Objective:** `FL-M46-01` — ปฏิบัติตามคำแนะนำในคู่มือการใช้งานต่าง ๆ คำชี้แจง คำอธิบาย และคำบรรยายที่ฟังและอ่าน
**Learning outcomes:** Learners follow multi-step procedures, identify conditions and confirm completion accurately.
**Practical activity:** Analyse a short equipment or online-service manual. Sequence actions, highlight warnings and demonstrate the procedure safely.
- **Differentiation:** M4 uses structured planning and source prompts; M5 increases independent comparison and explanation; M6 expects sustained independent judgement, accurate citation and audience-aware communication.

**Independent practice**
1. A manual says: “Save your work before restarting.” What must happen first? **Answer:** Save the work before restarting.
2. A procedure lists: connect, switch on, sign in, open the file. Put the steps in order. **Answer:** Connect; switch on; sign in; open the file.
3. Rewrite “You must not share your password” as a clear instruction. **Answer:** Do not share your password.
