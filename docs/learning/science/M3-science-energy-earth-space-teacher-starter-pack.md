# M3 Science — Energy, Light, Astronomy and Earth Science Teacher Starter Pack

**Curriculum:** Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), revised B.E. 2560 (2017)  
**Subject:** SCI  
**Coverage:** 21 official indicators in ว 2.3 and four in ว 3.1. The nine current M3 technology indicators in ว 4.1 and ว 4.2 are covered in the separate M3 technology teacher pack. The nine legacy W3.2 Earth-science lessons have been preserved in a supplementary pack and are not included in the OBEC 2021 core import.

Each lesson maps to one official indicator and includes three practice questions with answer keys. Electrical activities must use teacher-approved low-voltage supplies; no mains electricity. Never look directly at the Sun or direct lasers at eyes. Hazard lessons use official safety guidance and classroom case studies, not field visits to dangerous locations.


## Lesson 1 — Apply Ohm's law

**Objective:** `M3-S2_3-01`  \n**Official indicator:** ว 2.3 ม.3/1  \n**Priority classification (OBEC 2021):** must_know
**Learning outcome:** Use V = IR to solve for voltage, current or resistance.

**Teaching activity:** Use diagrams, models, supplied datasets and safe teacher-supervised demonstrations. Ask learners to show working, explain evidence and connect concepts to local examples.

**Independent practice**
1. What does R represent?  
   **Answer:** Resistance.
2. If I = 2 A and R = 3 Ω, what is V?  
   **Answer:** 6 V.
3. What happens to current if voltage rises while resistance stays constant?  
   **Answer:** It rises proportionally.
