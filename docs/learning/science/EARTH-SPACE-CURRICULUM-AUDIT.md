The official IPST manual lists the 13 M5 standard 2 outcomes in its curriculum alignment pages (printed pp. 21–24). Outcomes 12–13 cover weather-map symbols and basic weather analysis/forecasting.

## Outcome register

1. Uneven solar energy receipt/emission and regional air-temperature differences.
2. Earth's energy balance.
3. Pressure-gradient, Coriolis, centripetal and friction forces in atmospheric circulation.
4. Latitudinal atmospheric circulation and climate.
5. Ocean-water stratification.
6. Ocean circulation drivers and patterns.
7. Effects of ocean circulation on weather, organisms and environment.
8. Atmospheric stability and cloud formation.
9. Weather fronts and associated conditions.
10. Natural and human drivers of global climate change, supported by evidence.
11. Climate-change impacts and human practices that can slow climate change.
12. Weather-map symbols.
13. Weather analysis and basic forecasting from maps and information sources.

## Teaching content

The M5 teacher starter pack contains 13 lesson starters and 39 answer-keyed questions. Activities use weather maps, official weather observations, radar/satellite examples, climate datasets and simulations. Students are not asked to approach storms or observe lightning outdoors.
