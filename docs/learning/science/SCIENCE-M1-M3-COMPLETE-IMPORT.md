## Lesson starters

## Lesson 1 — [M1] Compare plant and animal cells

**Objective:** `M1-S1_2-01`  \n**Official indicator:** ว 1.2 ม.1/1  \n**Priority classification (OBEC 2021):** must_know
**Suggested duration:** 45–60 minutes  
**Learning outcome:** Compare plant and animal cells  
**Teaching activity:** Compare labelled diagrams or prepared slides. Match cell wall, membrane, cytoplasm, nucleus, vacuole, mitochondria and chloroplast to functions.

**Independent practice**
1. Name three structures in both plant and animal cells.  
   **Answer:** Cell membrane, cytoplasm and nucleus.
2. Name two structures usually found in plant cells but not animal cells.  
   **Answer:** Cell wall and chloroplast.
3. What does the nucleus do?  
   **Answer:** Controls cell activities.

**Teaching activity:** Demonstrate safe handling and low-power focusing; students draw and label a prepared specimen.

**Independent practice**
1. Which objective lens should be used first?  
   **Answer:** The lowest-power objective.
2. What should be adjusted if the image is unclear?  
   **Answer:** Focus and light, carefully.
3. Why carry a microscope with two hands?  
   **Answer:** To prevent dropping and damage.

## Lesson 5 — [M1] Explain diffusion and osmosis

**Objective:** `M1-S1_2-05`  \n**Official indicator:** ว 1.2 ม.1/5  \n**Priority classification (OBEC 2021):** must_know

**Teaching activity:** Model diffusion with food colouring and osmosis with potato strips in different solutions; do not taste materials.

**Independent practice**
1. In which direction does diffusion occur overall?

2. What moves during osmosis?  
   **Answer:** Water through a selectively permeable membrane.
3. Why do potato strips change in different solutions?  
   **Answer:** Water enters or leaves the cells depending on solution concentration.

**Curriculum:** Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), revised B.E. 2560 (2017)

## Lesson 111 — [M3] Apply Ohm's law

**Objective:** `M3-S2_3-01`  \n**Official indicator:** ว 2.3 ม.3/1  \n**Priority classification (OBEC 2021):** must_know
**Learning outcome:** Use V = IR to solve for voltage, current or resistance.

**Teaching activity:** Use diagrams, models, supplied datasets and safe teacher-supervised demonstrations. Ask learners to show working, explain evidence and connect concepts to local examples.

**Independent practice**
1. What does R represent?  
   **Answer:** Resistance.
2. If I = 2 A and R = 3 Ω, what is V?  
   **Answer:** 6 V.
3. What happens to current if voltage rises while resistance stays constant?  
   **Answer:** It rises proportionally.
