# M4 Biology — Cell Biology and Scientific Foundations Teacher Starter Pack

**Curriculum:** Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), revised B.E. 2560 (2017), Additional Biology strand 1  
**Subject:** BIOLOGY  
**Grade:** M4  
**Coverage:** 17 official additional-study learning outcomes (not basic-course indicators), pp. 134–139 of the OBEC/IPST curriculum document.

Each lesson aligns to one official M4 learning outcome and includes three practice questions with answer keys. Use appropriate microscope handling and biological specimen preparation. Follow institutional ethics and safety procedures; do not conduct experiments involving human subjects or vertebrate animals without formal approval.


## Lesson 1 — Explain characteristics and organisation of living things

**Objective:** `M4-BIO-01`  
**Official source alignment:** Additional Biology, strand 1, M4 learning outcome 1  
**Learning outcome:** Explain how characteristics such as nutrition, energy use, growth, response, homeostasis, reproduction and adaptation support life; relate cells, tissues, organs and systems.

**Teaching activity:** Use diagrams, models, safe microscopy, supplied datasets and teacher-supervised demonstrations. Ask learners to explain evidence, compare mechanisms and show calculations where appropriate.

**Independent practice**
1. Name two characteristics shared by living things.  
   **Answer:** They use energy and nutrients, grow, respond to stimuli, maintain internal balance and reproduce.
2. Arrange these levels from smallest to largest: organ, cell, organism, tissue.  
   **Answer:** Cell, tissue, organ, organism.
3. Why must organ systems work together?  
   **Answer:** Their coordinated functions maintain the organism's life processes.

   **Answer:** A reaction that requires an input of energy.

1. Which transport process moves particles down a concentration gradient without ATP?  
   **Answer:** Simple diffusion.

3. Why does active transport require energy?  
   **Answer:** It moves substances against their electrochemical gradient.


## Lesson 15 — Explain endocytosis and exocytosis

**Objective:** `M4-BIO-15`  
**Official source alignment:** Additional Biology, strand 1, M4 learning outcome 15  
**Learning outcome:** Diagram vesicle-mediated transport of large materials into and out of cells.

**Teaching activity:** Use diagrams, models, safe microscopy, supplied datasets and teacher-supervised demonstrations. Ask learners to explain evidence, compare mechanisms and show calculations where appropriate.

**Independent practice**
1. What is endocytosis?  
   **Answer:** A process in which the cell membrane encloses material to bring it into the cell in a vesicle.
2. What is exocytosis?  
   **Answer:** A vesicle fuses with the plasma membrane and releases contents outside the cell.
3. Name two types of endocytosis.  
   **Answer:** Phagocytosis and pinocytosis (also receptor-mediated endocytosis).

**Independent practice**
1. How many daughter cells usually result from mitosis?  
   **Answer:** Two genetically similar daughter cells.
2. How many daughter cells usually result from meiosis?  
   **Answer:** Four haploid cells, in the typical animal example.
3. Why is meiosis important?  
   **Answer:** It produces haploid gametes and contributes to genetic variation.
