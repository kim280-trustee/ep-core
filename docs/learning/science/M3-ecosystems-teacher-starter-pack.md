# M3 Science — Ecosystems and Energy Flow Teacher Starter Pack

**Curriculum:** Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), revised 2017  
**Source:** OBEC / IPST, *Indicators and Core Learning Content: Must-Know and Should-Know* (2021), M3 page 127.  
**Coverage:** Six official indicators under standard ว 1.1. Each lesson includes one activity and three answer-keyed formative questions.

## Lesson 1 — Explain interactions among ecosystem components

**Objective:** `M3-S1_1-01`  
**Official indicator:** ว 1.1 ม.3/1  
**Priority:** Must-Know  
**Activity:** Survey a school garden or use a prepared ecosystem picture. Sort living and non-living components, then draw arrows showing their interactions.

**Formative questions**
1. Name two living and two non-living ecosystem components. **Answer:** Plants and animals; light and water
2. Why do plants need sunlight and water? **Answer:** They use light energy and water in photosynthesis
3. How can temperature affect organisms? **Answer:** It affects growth, activity and survival

## Lesson 2 — Explain relationships among organisms

**Objective:** `M3-S1_1-02`  
**Official indicator:** ว 1.1 ม.3/2  
**Priority:** Should-Know  
**Activity:** Give groups organism cards and ask them to classify examples as mutualism, commensalism, predation or parasitism.

**Formative questions**
1. What is predation? **Answer:** One organism hunts and eats another
2. Give an example of mutualism. **Answer:** Bees obtain nectar while flowers are pollinated
3. What is a population? **Answer:** Members of the same species living in the same area at the same time

## Lesson 3 — Model energy transfer in food webs

**Objective:** `M3-S1_1-03`  
**Official indicator:** ว 1.1 ม.3/3  
**Priority:** Must-Know  
**Activity:** Build a food web using local organisms and arrows to show the direction of energy transfer.

**Formative questions**
1. Where does most energy in a food web begin? **Answer:** The Sun
2. What does an arrow in a food chain show? **Answer:** The direction energy moves from food to consumer
3. What may happen if one important prey species disappears? **Answer:** Predator populations may fall and the food web may change

## Lesson 4 — Explain producers, consumers and decomposers

**Objective:** `M3-S1_1-04`  
**Official indicator:** ว 1.1 ม.3/4  
**Priority:** Should-Know  
**Activity:** Sort organisms into producers, consumers and decomposers; explain how nutrients return to the environment.

**Formative questions**
1. What is a producer? **Answer:** An organism that makes its own food, usually by photosynthesis
2. Give one decomposer. **Answer:** A fungus or bacterium
3. Why are decomposers important? **Answer:** They break down dead material and return nutrients to the environment

## Lesson 5 — Explain toxin accumulation in food chains

**Objective:** `M3-S1_1-05`  
**Official indicator:** ว 1.1 ม.3/5  
**Priority:** Should-Know  
**Activity:** Use a teacher-prepared food-chain diagram to trace how a persistent pollutant can accumulate at higher trophic levels.

**Formative questions**
1. What is a food chain? **Answer:** A sequence showing who eats whom
2. Why can top predators have high pollutant concentrations? **Answer:** They may consume many contaminated organisms and accumulate persistent substances
3. Name one way to reduce this risk. **Answer:** Prevent pollutants from entering waterways and food webs

## Lesson 6 — Protect ecosystem balance

**Objective:** `M3-S1_1-06`  
**Official indicator:** ว 1.1 ม.3/6  
**Priority:** Should-Know  
**Activity:** Review a local environmental case and propose practical actions that protect organisms and ecosystem balance.

**Formative questions**
1. Give one human activity that can damage ecosystem balance. **Answer:** Deforestation or water pollution
2. Suggest one action a school can take. **Answer:** Reduce waste, protect habitat or plant suitable native species
3. Why should conservation involve the community? **Answer:** Local people help protect and manage resources over time

## Teacher-use notes

- Use local examples and prepared diagrams; do not disturb wildlife or damage habitats during surveys.
- Do not collect or handle unknown pollutants. Use hypothetical datasets for toxin-accumulation activities.
- OBEC 2021 page 127 marks ว 1.1 ม.3/6 as Should-Know. This has been visually verified and applied in both curriculum catalogues.
