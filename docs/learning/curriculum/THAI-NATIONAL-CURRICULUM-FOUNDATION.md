# Thai National Curriculum Foundation — E&P Learning

## Purpose

This document defines the official curriculum source register and implementation rules for Thailand. The national curriculum is the learning-outcome foundation; textbooks are separate resources mapped to those outcomes. A textbook upload must never silently create or replace national curriculum indicators.

## Curriculum versions kept separate

| Curriculum code | Official source | Grades | Platform status |
|---|---|---|---|
| `TH-BASIC-CORE-2008-2017` | Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), applicable 2017 revisions and later OBEC orders | P1–P6, M1–M6 | Draft foundation |
| `TH-PRIMARY-2025-P1-P3` | Primary Education Curriculum B.E. 2568 (2025), competency-based early-primary framework | P1–P3 | Draft; 24 annual capability outcomes and 25 illustrative knowledge topics seeded |

The 2025 P1–P3 framework is deliberately separate. Do not merge its outcomes into the 2008/2017 framework. OBEC publishes eight capability outcomes per grade, not eight replacement subjects. The official knowledge foundations are illustrative and may be adapted by schools.

## Eight learning areas in the 2008/2017 framework

1. Thai Language — ภาษาไทย
2. Mathematics — คณิตศาสตร์ (2017 revised standards)
3. Science and Technology — วิทยาศาสตร์และเทคโนโลยี (2017 revised standards)
4. Social Studies, Religion and Culture — สังคมศึกษา ศาสนา และวัฒนธรรม (Geography revised in 2017)
5. Health and Physical Education — สุขศึกษาและพลศึกษา
6. Arts — ศิลปะ
7. Career — การงานอาชีพ
8. Foreign Languages — ภาษาต่างประเทศ

### Important 2017/2018 technology change

OBEC Order 921/2561 removed the Design and Technology and ICT strands from the former Occupations and Technology learning area; these technology standards were moved into the Science learning area as Science and Technology standards ว 4.1 and ว 4.2. The Career learning area therefore remains separate. Do not treat Cambridge Click Start Computer Studies as a substitute for the Thai Science and Technology indicators.

The 2008/2017 national master now contains 1,940 unique official indicators and 2,334 grade alignments across all eight learning areas. Its catalogue remains **draft** pending consolidated source review and final application release testing; do not mark it active merely because the catalogue is populated.

## Thai Language — M1 to M3 implementation

- Official source: OBEC Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), Thai Language section, printed pages 45–55.
- Five standards registered: TH1.1 Reading, TH2.1 Writing, TH3.1 Listening/Viewing/Speaking, TH4.1 Principles of Thai Language Usage, and TH5.1 Literature and Literary Works.
- 101 official Thai indicator records are aligned to M1, M2 and M3: TH1.1 (25), TH2.1 (27), TH3.1 (18), TH4.1 (17), TH5.1 (14).
- Upper-secondary M4–M6 indicators are a grade-span set in the official curriculum and will be mapped separately; P1–P6 are also pending.

## Indicator transcription rules

- Preserve the official Thai indicator code exactly.
- Store the official Thai text and a separately reviewed English translation; never invent a translation and label it official.
- Preserve grade-level indicators for P1–M3 and interval indicators for M4–M6 exactly as the official framework defines them.
- Record source document title, B.E. year/version, page, learning area, strand, standard and indicator code.
- For the 2017 revision, preserve the revised Mathematics, Science and Geography scope; do not fall back to superseded 2008 indicators where the revision applies.
- Keep the Science and Technology ว 4.1 / ว 4.2 outcomes in Science and Technology, not Career.
- Keep textbook chapters, lesson plans, practice questions and worksheets as separate teaching resources linked to one or more official indicators.
- Do not copy substantial copyrighted textbook prose or exercises. Create original teaching materials and retain bibliographic references.
