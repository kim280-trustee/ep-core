# Health & Physical Education M4–M6 — Shared Indicators — Teacher Starter Pack

**Curriculum:** Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), revised B.E. 2560 (2017)  
**Subject:** Health and Physical Education (`HEALTH-PE`)  
**Grade coverage:** M4, M5 and M6 (29 shared indicators)  
**Unique indicators:** 29  
**OBEC 2021 priority:** 12 must-know; 17 should-know  
**Assessment material:** 87 formative prompts with expected-answer guidance

This pack preserves the official Thai indicator wording and standard code. Each lesson includes a practical teaching activity and three formative assessment prompts. Practical performance should be assessed through observable evidence, not written recall alone.

## Teaching and safeguarding guidance

- Adapt activities to learner age, ability, disability and school facilities; provide safe alternatives.
- Use warm-ups, suitable equipment, safe surfaces, hydration and clear stop signals for physical activities.
- Use fictional scenarios for relationship, puberty, sexual-health, substance-use and violence topics. Never require personal disclosures.
- Use school-approved health information. For first aid or CPR, use trained supervision and mannequins; do not practise CPR on classmates.
- Treat answer guidance as examples of acceptable evidence. Accept other accurate, safe and curriculum-aligned responses.

Learners produce one observable piece of evidence directly linked to the indicator.

**Practical activity:** At upper-secondary level, use a visual model, structured discussion and guided practice focused on body systems, growth and development. Learners plan and justify an approach, monitor results, evaluate limitations and explain implications for a team, family or community where relevant. **Teacher check:** Score three criteria separately: (1) accuracy against “อธิบายกระบวนการสร้างเสริมและดำรงประสิทธิภาพการทำงานของระบบอวัยวะต่าง ๆ”; (2) appropriate application of body systems, growth and development; and (3) safe, observable evidence at upper-secondary level. Give two specific facts, decisions or steps that would demonstrate this outcome. **Answer:** The response must give two accurate, distinct points directly relevant to “อธิบายกระบวนการสร้างเสริมและดำรงประสิทธิภาพการทำงานของระบบอวัยวะต่าง ๆ”, correctly identify the essential concept or action in body systems, growth and development, and explain its relevance. 2. **Apply it to a case:** A fictional learner is given a labelled body-system diagram and a short description of a normal age-related change involving body systems, growth and development. Ask the learner to interpret the diagram, explain the relevant function and identify an appropriate health-support action without making a diagnosis. What should the learner do first, what should happen next, and why does that response meet “อธิบายกระบวนการสร้างเสริมและดำรงประสิทธิภาพการทำงานของระบบอวัยวะต่าง ๆ”? **Answer:** The learner selects a safe and relevant action for body systems, growth and development, explains the reason and explicitly links the decision to the official outcome “อธิบายกระบวนการสร้างเสริมและดำรงประสิทธิภาพการทำงานของระบบอวัยวะต่าง ๆ”. For health or safety concerns, the response must not diagnose or prescribe treatment; it must refer to a responsible adult or trained service when appropriate. Sensitive relationship topics must remain fictional and protect privacy.
3. **Show the learning:** Produce a short demonstration, annotated diagram, decision pathway, performance record or action plan that proves “อธิบายกระบวนการสร้างเสริมและดำรงประสิทธิภาพการทำงานของระบบอวัยวะต่าง ๆ”. Include (a) the key steps or features, (b) one observable success criterion, and (c) an explanation of how the evidence meets the outcome. **Answer:** The evidence must be specific to “อธิบายกระบวนการสร้างเสริมและดำรงประสิทธิภาพการทำงานของระบบอวัยวะต่าง ๆ”, include the relevant features of body systems, growth and development, and satisfy all three requirements. Assess practical movement through safe observable performance, sensitive topics through a fictional response, and first aid/CPR only through trained supervision and appropriate mannequins.
