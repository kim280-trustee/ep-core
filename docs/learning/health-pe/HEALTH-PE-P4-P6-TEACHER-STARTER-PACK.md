# Health & Physical Education P4-P6 — Teacher Starter Pack

**Curriculum:** Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), revised B.E. 2560 (2017)  
**Subject:** Health and Physical Education (`HEALTH-PE`)  
**Grade coverage:** P4-P6  
**Unique indicators:** 66  
**OBEC 2021 priority:** 31 must-know; 35 should-know  
**Assessment material:** 198 formative prompts with expected-answer guidance

This pack preserves the official Thai indicator wording and standard code. Each lesson includes a practical teaching activity and three formative assessment prompts. Practical performance should be assessed through observable evidence, not written recall alone.

## Teaching and safeguarding guidance

- Adapt activities to learner age, ability, disability and school facilities; provide safe alternatives.
- Use warm-ups, suitable equipment, safe surfaces, hydration and clear stop signals for physical activities.
- Use fictional scenarios for relationship, puberty, sexual-health, substance-use and violence topics. Never require personal disclosures.
- Use school-approved health information. For first aid or CPR, use trained supervision and mannequins; do not practise CPR on classmates.
- Treat answer guidance as examples of acceptable evidence. Accept other accurate, safe and curriculum-aligned responses.

### 1. HPE-P4-01 — อธิบายการเจริญเติบโตและพัฒนาการของร่างกายและจิตใจตามวัย

- **Official indicator:** `พ 1.1 ป.4/1`
- **Standard:** พ 1.1 — Growth, development and body systems
- **OBEC 2021 priority:** `must_know`
- **Original Thai indicator:** อธิบายการเจริญเติบโตและพัฒนาการของร่างกายและจิตใจตามวัย

**Objective:** `HPE-P4-01` — อธิบายการเจริญเติบโตและพัฒนาการของร่างกายและจิตใจตามวัย

**Teaching activity:** Use a visual model, structured discussion and guided practice focused on body growth and development. Learners explain the key concept, apply it to a familiar fictional example and produce one observable piece of evidence.

**Practical activity:** Use a visual model, structured discussion and guided practice focused on body growth and development. Learners explain the key concept, apply it to a familiar fictional example and produce one observable piece of evidence. Each learner must produce individual evidence of the target skill or decision; provide an accessible alternative that measures the same outcome.

**Expected evidence:** Evidence must directly demonstrate “อธิบายการเจริญเติบโตและพัฒนาการของร่างกายและจิตใจตามวัย”. Accept evidence showing accurate subject knowledge, a relevant example, a safe practical application, and a clear explanation. The learner must explain why the evidence is correct for body growth and development at upper-primary level; do not award full credit for unrelated general advice.

**Teacher check:** Score three criteria separately: (1) accuracy against “อธิบายการเจริญเติบโตและพัฒนาการของร่างกายและจิตใจตามวัย”; (2) appropriate application of body growth and development; and (3) safe, observable evidence at upper-primary level. Record specific feedback for each criterion, accept equivalent accessible demonstrations and correct misconceptions.

**Independent practice**
1. **Knowledge and understanding:** The official outcome is “อธิบายการเจริญเติบโตและพัฒนาการของร่างกายและจิตใจตามวัย”. In your own words, explain what a learner must know or be able to do for body growth and development. Give two specific facts, decisions or steps that would demonstrate this outcome. **Answer:** The response must give two accurate, distinct points directly relevant to “อธิบายการเจริญเติบโตและพัฒนาการของร่างกายและจิตใจตามวัย”, correctly identify the essential concept or action in body growth and development, and explain its relevance. Accept equivalent accurate wording, but not unrelated general statements.
2. **Apply it to a case:** A fictional learner is given a labelled body-system diagram and a short description of a normal age-related change involving body growth and development. Ask the learner to interpret the diagram, explain the relevant function and identify an appropriate health-support action without making a diagnosis. What should the learner do first, what should happen next, and why does that response meet “อธิบายการเจริญเติบโตและพัฒนาการของร่างกายและจิตใจตามวัย”? **Answer:** The learner selects a safe and relevant action for body growth and development, explains the reason and explicitly links the decision to the official outcome “อธิบายการเจริญเติบโตและพัฒนาการของร่างกายและจิตใจตามวัย”. For health or safety concerns, the response must not diagnose or prescribe treatment; it must refer to a responsible adult or trained service when appropriate. Sensitive relationship topics must remain fictional and protect privacy.
3. **Show the learning:** Produce a short demonstration, annotated diagram, decision pathway, performance record or action plan that proves “อธิบายการเจริญเติบโตและพัฒนาการของร่างกายและจิตใจตามวัย”. Include (a) the key steps or features, (b) one observable success criterion, and (c) an explanation of how the evidence meets the outcome. **Answer:** The evidence must be specific to “อธิบายการเจริญเติบโตและพัฒนาการของร่างกายและจิตใจตามวัย”, include the relevant features of body growth and development, and satisfy all three requirements. Assess practical movement through safe observable performance, sensitive topics through a fictional response, and first aid/CPR only through trained supervision and appropriate mannequins.
