# Health & Physical Education P1-P3 — Teacher Starter Pack

**Curriculum:** Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), revised B.E. 2560 (2017)  
**Subject:** Health and Physical Education (`HEALTH-PE`)  
**Grade coverage:** P1-P3  
**Unique indicators:** 54  
**OBEC 2021 priority:** 28 must-know; 26 should-know  
**Assessment material:** 162 formative prompts with expected-answer guidance

This pack preserves the official Thai indicator wording and standard code. Each lesson includes a practical teaching activity and three formative assessment prompts. Practical performance should be assessed through observable evidence, not written recall alone.

## Teaching and safeguarding guidance

- Adapt activities to learner age, ability, disability and school facilities; provide safe alternatives.
- Use warm-ups, suitable equipment, safe surfaces, hydration and clear stop signals for physical activities.
- Use fictional scenarios for relationship, puberty, sexual-health, substance-use and violence topics. Never require personal disclosures.
- Use school-approved health information. For first aid or CPR, use trained supervision and mannequins; do not practise CPR on classmates.
- Treat answer guidance as examples of acceptable evidence. Accept other accurate, safe and curriculum-aligned responses.

### 1. HPE-P1-01 — อธิบายลักษณะและหน้าที่ของอวัยวะภายนอก

- **Official indicator:** `พ 1.1 ป.1/1`
- **Standard:** พ 1.1 — Growth, development and body systems
- **OBEC 2021 priority:** `should_know`
- **Original Thai indicator:** อธิบายลักษณะและหน้าที่ของอวัยวะภายนอก

**Objective:** `HPE-P1-01` — อธิบายลักษณะและหน้าที่ของอวัยวะภายนอก

**Teaching activity:** Use a visual model, structured discussion and guided practice focused on external body organs. Learners explain the key concept, apply it to a familiar fictional example and produce one observable piece of evidence.

**Practical activity:** Use a visual model, structured discussion and guided practice focused on external body organs. Learners explain the key concept, apply it to a familiar fictional example and produce one observable piece of evidence. Each learner must produce individual evidence of the target skill or decision; provide an accessible alternative that measures the same outcome.

**Expected evidence:** Evidence must directly demonstrate “อธิบายลักษณะและหน้าที่ของอวัยวะภายนอก”. Accept evidence showing accurate subject knowledge, a relevant example, a safe practical application, and a clear explanation. The learner must explain why the evidence is correct for external body organs at early-primary level; do not award full credit for unrelated general advice.

**Teacher check:** Score three criteria separately: (1) accuracy against “อธิบายลักษณะและหน้าที่ของอวัยวะภายนอก”; (2) appropriate application of external body organs; and (3) safe, observable evidence at early-primary level. Record specific feedback for each criterion, accept equivalent accessible demonstrations and correct misconceptions.

**Independent practice**
1. **Knowledge and understanding:** The official outcome is “อธิบายลักษณะและหน้าที่ของอวัยวะภายนอก”. In your own words, explain what a learner must know or be able to do for external body organs. Give two specific facts, decisions or steps that would demonstrate this outcome. **Answer:** The response must give two accurate, distinct points directly relevant to “อธิบายลักษณะและหน้าที่ของอวัยวะภายนอก”, correctly identify the essential concept or action in external body organs, and explain its relevance. Accept equivalent accurate wording, but not unrelated general statements.
2. **Apply it to a case:** A fictional learner is given a labelled body-system diagram and a short description of a normal age-related change involving external body organs. Ask the learner to interpret the diagram, explain the relevant function and identify an appropriate health-support action without making a diagnosis. What should the learner do first, what should happen next, and why does that response meet “อธิบายลักษณะและหน้าที่ของอวัยวะภายนอก”? **Answer:** The learner selects a safe and relevant action for external body organs, explains the reason and explicitly links the decision to the stated outcome. For health or safety concerns, the response must not diagnose or prescribe treatment; it must refer to a responsible adult or trained service when appropriate. Sensitive relationship topics must remain fictional and protect privacy.
3. **Show the learning:** Produce a short demonstration, annotated diagram, decision pathway, performance record or action plan that proves “อธิบายลักษณะและหน้าที่ของอวัยวะภายนอก”. Include (a) the key steps or features, (b) one observable success criterion, and (c) an explanation of how the evidence meets the outcome. **Answer:** The evidence must be specific to “อธิบายลักษณะและหน้าที่ของอวัยวะภายนอก”, include the relevant features of external body organs, and satisfy all three requirements. Assess practical movement through safe observable performance, sensitive topics through a fictional response, and first aid/CPR only through trained supervision and appropriate mannequins.
