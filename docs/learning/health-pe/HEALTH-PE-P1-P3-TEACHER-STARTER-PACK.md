# Health & Physical Education P1-P3 — Teacher Starter Pack

**Curriculum:** Thai Basic Education Core Curriculum B.E. 2551 (2008), revised B.E. 2560 (2017)  
**Subject:** Health and Physical Education (`HEALTH-PE`)  
**Grade coverage:** P1-P3  
**Unique indicators:** 54  
**OBEC 2021 priority:** 28 must-know; 26 should-know  
**Assessment material:** 162 formative prompts with expected-answer guidance

This pack preserves the official Thai indicator wording and standard code. Each lesson includes a practical teaching activity and three formative assessment prompts. Practical performance should be assessed through observable evidence, not written recall alone.

## Teaching and safeguarding guidance

- Adapt activities to learner age, ability, disability and school facilities; provide safe alternatives.
- Use warm-ups, suitable equipment, safe surfaces, hydration and clear stop signals for physical activities.
- Use fictional scenarios for relationship, puberty, sexual-health, substance-use and violence topics. Never require personal disclosures.
- Use school-approved health information. For first aid or CPR, use trained supervision and mannequins; do not practise CPR on classmates.
- Treat answer guidance as examples of acceptable evidence. Accept other accurate, safe and curriculum-aligned responses.

## Indicator lessons

### 1. HPE-P1-01 — อธิบายลักษณะและหน้าที่ของอวัยวะภายนอก

- **Official indicator:** `พ 1.1 ป.1/1`
- **Standard:** พ 1.1 — Growth, development and body systems
- **OBEC 2021 priority:** `should_know`
- **Original Thai indicator:** อธิบายลักษณะและหน้าที่ของอวัยวะภายนอก

**Teaching activity:** Use body-picture cards, simple models and guided movement. Learners identify the body feature or growth idea in the indicator and practise one age-appropriate care routine.

**Formative assessment and answer guidance**

1. **Knowledge check — What body feature, system, growth change or development idea is named in this indicator?**  
   **Expected answer/evidence:** The learner identifies the feature or concept accurately and explains its role or relevance.
2. **Application — Give one age-appropriate way to care for or support the body feature or development issue in the indicator.**  
   **Expected answer/evidence:** A safe, practical self-care action directly related to the indicator.
3. **Evidence — Explain or demonstrate one action that shows understanding of this indicator.**  
   **Expected answer/evidence:** The learner gives an accurate explanation or demonstrates the relevant care behaviour safely.

- **Official indicator:** `พ 3.1 ป.1/1`
- **Standard:** พ 3.1 — Movement skills and sport performance
- **OBEC 2021 priority:** `must_know`
- **Original Thai indicator:** เคลื่อนไหวร่างกายขณะอยู่กับที่ เคลื่อนที่และใช้อุปกรณ์ประกอบ

**Teaching activity:** Set up safe skill stations with clear demonstrations. Learners practise the movement or sport skill progressively, receive feedback and repeat it with control, coordination and suitable equipment.

**Formative assessment and answer guidance**

1. **Knowledge check — Name the movement pattern, motor skill or sport skill targeted by this indicator.**  
   **Expected answer/evidence:** The learner identifies the target skill and its key movement components.
2. **Application — What should the learner adjust to perform the skill with better control?**  
   **Expected answer/evidence:** A relevant point such as balance, posture, timing, direction, force, coordination or object control.
3. **Practical evidence — Demonstrate the skill in a safe practice space.**  
   **Expected answer/evidence:** The teacher observes controlled movement, appropriate technique, coordination and safe use of space or equipment.

   **Expected answer/evidence:** A realistic self-assessment based on effort, skill, teamwork or fitness progress.
